> [!info] Brief Description
> A theater-based game where the group creates and analyzes frozen tableaux based on given themes.

**Group Size**: 5-25 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Duration**: 10-20 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A theater/circus pedagogy game for small to medium-sized groups.

The group moves freely around the playing area. On a signal, the task is to represent a theme (with movements frozen) in the center of the space.
The finished tableau is photographed.

After a few rounds, the group sits down together to analyze the images: the composition, the distribution of people in the space, their visibility, etc.

Possible themes: Fairy tales, circus, cleaning, office life, shopping, playground, at the beach, etc.
