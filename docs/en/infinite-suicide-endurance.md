> [!info] Short Description
> Advanced diabolists compete to keep an infinite suicide going longer than the others.

**Group Size**: 3 to 60 players  
**Difficulty**: schwer  
**Material**: Diabolo and handsticks  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Advanced diabolists compete to keep an infinite suicide going longer than the others. If several players are too stable, add extra tasks such as standing on one leg, turning, hopping or walking while maintaining the trick.

## Variations

Only use this with players who already know the trick well.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
