# Circus Klatschmohn

Circus Klatschmohn offers weekly year-round classes for children, teenagers, and adults in its heated circus tents at Rossplatz. The program includes aerial arts, acrobatics, balancing, and juggling, among other disciplines.

A free trial class is available by prior arrangement with the circus office. As many classes are fully booked, there may be a waiting list.
