# Acirkaos Circo Social

ACIRKAOS Circo Social, based in Menorca, Spain, is a non-profit organization dedicated to promoting circus arts as tools for education, social inclusion, and community development. Their comprehensive pedagogical approach encourages participants to discover circus through play, stimulating imagination, physical skills, and performing arts, while emphasizing values education for children, youth, and adults.

A key focus of ACIRKAOS is its commitment to inclusivity, particularly through programs designed for individuals with disabilities. The organization has conducted workshops tailored for the Fundación de Personas con Discapacidad de Menorca, demonstrating their dedication to integrating individuals with disabilities into their activities.

ACIRKAOS also organizes European Social Circus Encounters, bringing together youth workers from various countries to share experiences and methodologies in social circus. These events include seminars, workshops, and performances, fostering an environment of learning and cultural exchange.
