# Imhotep Cirque

Imhotep Cirque, established in 1987 and located in Villenave d'Ornon, France, is dedicated to making circus arts accessible to individuals with disabilities. Their specialized workshops cater to children and adults with mental (e.g., autism, Down syndrome, intellectual disabilities), physical, and sensory challenges. The primary objectives of these programs are to enhance self-expression, build self-confidence, promote cooperation, and provide a safe, non-competitive environment for participants to explore circus activities. Experienced and certified instructors lead these sessions in accessible facilities equipped with various circus apparatus. Collaborations with requesting institutions ensure that each program is tailored to the specific needs of the participants, with small group sizes and appropriately timed sessions to maximize engagement and benefit.
