# Ludoardo (Eduardo Filaffero)

Eduardo offers circus activities for young people with disabilities. He provides 1:1 sessions, group workshops, and instructor training, focusing on people with physical and cognitive disabilities, learning disorders, and psychological challenges. Activities range from educational programs to team-building and performances, using functional juggling and adapted circus arts to promote inclusion, personal growth, and social connection.
