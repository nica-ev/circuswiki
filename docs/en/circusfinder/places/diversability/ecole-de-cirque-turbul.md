# Ecole de Cirque Turbul'

Ecole de Cirque Turbul’, run by the Appel d’Air association, champions inclusive, accessible circus for all. Through leisure classes and holiday stages, they teach disciplines like acrobatics, juggling, aerials, clowning, and mime to children, teens, and adults—without competition, fostering mutual aid and creative expression.

Their “cirque adapté” program partners with medico-social institutions to offer tailored workshops for people with physical or mental disabilities. These sessions promote psychomotor development, self-discovery, confidence, and social interaction in a non-judgmental, supportive environment.

As part of its community outreach, Turbul’ organizes Turbul’ en Chap’, a free annual event held in underserved neighborhoods with participatory circus workshops, collective performances, and public dialogue—bringing art, inclusion, and cultural exchange directly to diverse communities.
