# Zirku Topia e.V.

ZirkuTopia is an inclusive circus organization that offers a variety of activities, including weekly trainings, workshops, summer camps, adventure-based circus sessions, and international youth exchanges. Their approach emphasizes individual strengths, promoting fun, movement, and creativity.

They teach juggling, unicycling, acrobatics, and more, guided by the belief that everyone has unique talents to contribute. ZirkuTopia welcomes participants of all backgrounds and abilities, ensuring an inclusive environment where physical or mental disabilities are not barriers to participation.

Circus activities are held daily in their own circus tent, which is accessible via a paved path, allowing easy entry for all. Their commitment to accessibility and inclusion fosters a space where everyone can experience the joy of circus arts.
