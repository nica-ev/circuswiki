# Spazio Bizzarro

Spazio Bizzarro, established in 2012 by the Associazione Tra Le Nuvole, is a contemporary circus center located in Casatenovo, Lecco, Italy. It serves as a cultural hub offering courses, workshops, and artistic training in various circus disciplines for children, teenagers, and adults.

The center's curriculum encompasses a wide range of disciplines, including aerial acrobatics, juggling, balancing, clowning, dance, singing, and theater. These programs aim to foster creativity, self-expression, and physical fitness among participants.

Spazio Bizzarro is committed to inclusivity and social engagement, actively promoting diversity through activities that involve all individuals, ensuring that everyone can participate. They offer specialized programs such as social circus courses designed to include individuals with disabilities and those from disadvantaged backgrounds, utilizing circus arts as a means of social integration and personal development.

In addition to regular courses, Spazio Bizzarro organizes educational projects for schools, providing students with hands-on experience in circus arts through workshops and guided activities. They also host summer camps and professional training programs in aerial disciplines, supported by the Ministry of Culture and endorsed by the Associazione Circo Contemporaneo Italia (ACCI).
