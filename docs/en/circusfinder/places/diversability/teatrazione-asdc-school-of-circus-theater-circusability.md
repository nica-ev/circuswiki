# Teatrazione ASDC School Of Circus Theater / CircUsAbility

TeatrAzionE is a circus-theatre school based in Turin, Italy, offering a wide range of circus courses and performances for all age groups. In addition to its general training in aerial acrobatics, juggling, balancing, ground acrobatics, and clowning, the school runs specialized adapted circus programs designed for individuals with disabilities. These include targeted workshops for people with physical, intellectual, psychological, or sensory disabilities, as well as neurodevelopmental disorders.

TeatrAzionE also places strong emphasis on training future circus educators, particularly in the field of inclusive and adapted circus arts, equipping them with the skills to work with diverse populations. Their inclusive approach ensures that circus becomes a tool for self-expression, empowerment, and social integration for people of all abilities. Through its educational and artistic activities, TeatrAzionE promotes a vision of circus as a transformative, accessible, and creative experience for all.
