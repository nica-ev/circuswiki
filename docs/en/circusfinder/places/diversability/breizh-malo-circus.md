# Breizh Malo Circus

L’École de Cirque de Bretagne – Breizh Malo Circus, based in Saint-Malo, offers a wide range of circus arts activities for children, teenagers, and adults. Deeply committed to accessibility and inclusion, the school has developed a specific program for young people with disabilities. Through tailored workshops, they welcome children and youth from specialized institutions, adapting circus disciplines such as juggling, acrobatics, balancing, and aerial work to each participant’s abilities.

These inclusive sessions are held either in the school's circus tent or directly within specialized centers and aim to foster creativity, self-confidence, and motor coordination in a playful and supportive environment. The initiative is designed to allow every child, regardless of physical or cognitive ability, to experience the joy and empowerment of circus. In doing so, the École de Cirque de Bretagne contributes to greater social inclusion while promoting the values of cooperation, expression, and artistic discovery.
