# Varieté Sociále / Centrum duševního zdraví Plzeň, Ledovec

Varieté Sociále is a social circus initiative by Ledovec, z.s., aimed at promoting destigmatization and deinstitutionalization through interactive performances. Drawing from their extensive experience with the Cirkus Paciento project in psychiatric hospitals and social services, they offer collaborative circus-themed shows that actively involve both service users and staff. These performances are tailored to invigorate routines, foster creativity, and present organizations uniquely to the public. Emphasizing therapeutic benefits, participants are encouraged to explore roles beyond traditional patient or staff identities, leading to mutual discovery of hidden talents and capabilities. Varieté Sociále is available for events in psychiatric hospitals, residential, and outpatient social services, especially during festivals or open days that unite disadvantaged individuals with the broader community. Their flexible approach allows for rapid show creation or extended engagements for deeper therapeutic experiences, ensuring each performance aligns with the specific needs of the hosting service. Financial support from entities like the Ministry of Culture and the Život umělce foundation ensures accessibility for most services.
