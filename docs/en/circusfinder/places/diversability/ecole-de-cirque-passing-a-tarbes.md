# Ecole de cirque Passing à Tarbes

Passing is a welcoming circus school founded in 1999, offering accessible, playful circus arts for all ages and abilities. Under the values of respect, trust, collaboration, and diversity, they provide week-long workshops, year-round classes, and tailored stages (e.g., family or thematic sessions) that invite everyone to discover juggling, acrobatics, unicycling, aerial arts, and more—without performance pressure.

Their programs adapt to diverse audiences—schools, childcare centers, specialized institutions, families, and community groups—ensuring inclusive experiences that honor individual rhythms and creativity. With a strong collaborative spirit, Passing also brings circus to life across Hautes-Pyrénées towns like Vic-en-Bigorre, Pierrefitte-Nestalas, and Bagnères-de-Bigorre by traveling with their equipment and spreading joy through shared practice.

Through student-involved performances, community-driven projects, and expertise in non-competitive pedagogy, Passing's circus is truly for everyone—bridging people, cultures, and abilities through the art of playful movement.
