# Kejklíři Cascabel

Cascabel is a Czech non-profit organization based in Olomouc, dedicated to promoting circus arts among children and youth. They offer various programs, including juggling clubs and workshops, aimed at fostering creativity and skill development. Emphasizing inclusivity, Cascabel integrates children from diverse backgrounds, such as those in foster care and individuals with neurodevelopmental disorders like ADHD, into their activities alongside neurotypical peers. This approach not only enhances participants' social skills and self-confidence but also cultivates a supportive community where every child is valued and encouraged to thrive. Cascabel's commitment to inclusivity ensures that all participants, regardless of their personal challenges, have the opportunity to engage in and benefit from the enriching world of circus arts.
