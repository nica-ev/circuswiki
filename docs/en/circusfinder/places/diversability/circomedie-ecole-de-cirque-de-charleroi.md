# Circomédie / École de Cirque de Charleroi

The École de Cirque de Charleroi offers the "Circ'onstances" program, designed to introduce the art of circus to individuals with mental, behavioral, or psychiatric challenges. This initiative utilizes circus activities such as juggling, balance exercises, and expressive movement to foster personal development and social integration. The program emphasizes the process over performance, aiming to enhance body awareness, psychomotor skills, coordination, and spatial-temporal structuring. It also promotes group work, highlighting values like mutual aid and respect. Since September 2015, the school has been a member of the Ligue Handisport Francophone.
