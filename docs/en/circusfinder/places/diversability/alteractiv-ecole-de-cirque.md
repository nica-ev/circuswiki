# AlterActiv, École de Cirque

AlterActiv is a multicultural and sports association, offering a variety of programs, including circus arts and martial arts. Their École de Cirque provides annual courses, weekend sessions, and vacation workshops for individuals of all ages. In addition to general circus training, AlterActiv is committed to inclusivity through its "Handi Cirque - Cirque Adapté" program. This initiative offers adapted circus courses specifically designed for children with disabilities, accommodating those who may require the presence of a parent during sessions. The program aims to create an inclusive environment where children with disabilities can explore and enjoy circus arts alongside their peers.
