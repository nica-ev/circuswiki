# Armazém Aério - Academia e Companhia de Novo Circo

Armazém Aério is a distinguished Portuguese academy and contemporary circus company established in 2004, dedicated to promoting and teaching new circus arts. Operating in both Lisbon and Almada, the organization offers a variety of classes encompassing aerial acrobatics, trapeze, lyra, and other disciplines, catering to children, youth, and adults.

Since 2008, Armazém Aério has been committed to inclusivity through its Dance 21+ program, specifically designed for individuals with physical disabilities. This initiative integrates contemporary dance and new circus techniques, focusing on participants with Down syndrome (Trisomy 21) and extending a welcoming environment to all. The program emphasizes the potential of aerial acrobatics, enabling participants to perform movements on suspended apparatuses like trapeze and aerial fabrics. Through tailored instruction and support, students overcome challenges and achieve remarkable feats, exemplifying the transformative power of circus arts.
