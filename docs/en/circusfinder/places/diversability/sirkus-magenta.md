# Sirkus Magenta

A nonprofit, nonreligious, and nonpolitical organization, Sirkus Magenta operates locally, nationally, and internationally. The organization focuses on social circus, tailoring circus activities to participants' abilities. They run an annual performing group, where participants practice various circus disciplines like juggling, acrobatics, aerial arts, and clowning. The group, led by circus, theater, and music instructors, includes musicians with disabilities or on the autism spectrum. The program fosters self-expression, creativity, and social interaction. The end-of-season performance is free and open to the public, highlighting the work of people with developmental disabilities and autism. Sirkus Magenta’s inclusive approach uses a variety of circus tools and methods in a well-equipped circus space, serving young people with disabilities and autism aged 16-29.
