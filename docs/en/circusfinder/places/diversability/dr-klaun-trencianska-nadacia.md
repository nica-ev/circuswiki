# Dr.Klaun, Trenčianska Nadácia

**Dr. Clown Fund** is a Slovak initiative that, in cooperation with Kvart Art Agency, brings joy and emotional support to children with various illnesses and disabilities living in special facilities across Slovakia. These children, often separated from their families for extended periods, are cared for in social service homes, special boarding schools, and treatment centers. Since 2009, *Dr. Clowns*—a team of puppeteers, mimes, clowns, and magicians—have been visiting these places, offering around 100 unique performances per year. Their interactive shows create precious moments of laughter and emotional connection, helping children forget their isolation and challenges, even if only for a while. The program focuses especially on underserved areas where local support is limited, providing comfort and happiness to children who need it most. The work of Dr. Clown Fund is a powerful reminder of how art, empathy, and play can become essential tools for healing and inclusion.
