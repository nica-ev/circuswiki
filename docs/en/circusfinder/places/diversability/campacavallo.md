# Campacavallo

This organization offers circus and equestrian activities including workshops, educational initiatives, and summer camps. It uses small circus techniques, social circus pedagogy, and multidisciplinary activities to engage children and adolescents with disabilities or social and psychological challenges. Circus activities for young people with disabilities are tailored to each project, with a focus on inclusion and personal development. Activities are held based on the specific project needs.
