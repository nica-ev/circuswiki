# Asphyxie

Asphyxie Cirque, established in 2003 in Blanquefort, France, is dedicated to promoting circus and street arts through creation, education, and outreach. Among its diverse offerings, the association places a strong emphasis on inclusivity, particularly through its Handi-Cirque program designed for individuals with disabilities.

The Handi-Cirque workshops aim to make circus arts accessible to everyone, focusing on enhancing psychomotor skills and social interaction. Led by instructors certified with BPJEPS in circus activities and trained in adapted circus techniques, these sessions cover disciplines such as juggling, acrobatics, object balancing, and clowning. The primary goal is not to train professional circus artists but to use circus as an educational tool to foster self-expression, autonomy, creativity, and group dynamics.

Each workshop is tailored to accommodate the age and specific needs of participants, ensuring a safe and supportive environment. The program is structured into three progressive cycles: initiation, where participants are introduced to various circus disciplines; skill development, focusing on technical proficiency in chosen areas; and creation and presentation, culminating in a collective performance that showcases the participants' progress and creativity.
