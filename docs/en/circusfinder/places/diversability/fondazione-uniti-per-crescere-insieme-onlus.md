# Fondazione Uniti per crescere insieme ONLUS

Uniti per Crescere Insieme (UCI) is a non-profit organization based in Italy that leverages circus arts as a means of social transformation and community well-being, focusing on individuals in vulnerable situations. Their initiatives span across various cities, including Turin, Milan, and Bologna, encompassing projects in schools, hospitals, community centres, and public spaces.

One of their key programs, Circospedale, brings social and educational circus activities to pediatric hospital wards. Through disciplines like juggling, magic, and balance exploration, this project aims to enhance resilience, activate personal resources, and improve the well-being of young patients.

UCI also operates Casa Circostanza, Italy's only centre entirely dedicated to social circus, offering socio-educational pathways for children, adolescents, and individuals with disabilities. Additionally, their Scuola di Circostanza provides professional training in circus and social theatre, equipping participants with skills applicable in various professional contexts.
