# Bátor Tábor Alapítvány

**Bátor Tábor** is a foundation in Hungary providing therapeutic recreation programs for children with serious illnesses and their families. Their free programs include camps in Hatvan, hospital and school outreach, and online sessions. Circus activities form a key part of their therapeutic approach, helping children build self-confidence, patience, and joy through juggling, acrobatics, and aerial arts. These are also included in school outreach, promoting teamwork and community spirit. Their programs support children with cancer, hemophilia, chronic arthritis, diabetes, and other serious conditions. Bátor Tábor’s accessible camp facilities ensure safe participation for all children, regardless of their physical abilities.
