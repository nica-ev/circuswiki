# Par Haz'art- School Circus Adapted

Par Haz’Art is an inclusive circus school based in Balma, near Toulouse, France, dedicated to making circus arts accessible to all. A strong focus of the organisation is on adapted circus activities for children, teenagers, and adults with disabilities. Each week, Par Haz’Art runs adapted circus workshops in collaboration with nearly twenty specialized institutions, including IME and ITEP for children and youth with disabilities. These sessions combine fun and creativity with motor skill development, confidence building, and social inclusion. From 2021 to 2023, Par Haz’Art led “Évasions Circassiennes,” a project supported by the ARS/DRAC Culture and Handicap scheme, which explored artistic creation in adapted circus and culminated in inclusive performances. With about 100 participants involved each week, Par Haz’Art is a reference in France for inclusive and socially engaged circus practice, proving that circus can be a powerful tool for expression and empowerment for young people with disabilities.
