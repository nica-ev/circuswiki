# Spaziocirco

Spazio Circo is an Italian association committed to promoting circus arts as a tool for education, personal development, and social inclusion. It offers a wide range of programs tailored to different age groups and contexts. These include regular circus courses held at their facilities for children, teenagers, and adults, as well as workshops in schools, communities, and businesses aimed at introducing the basics of circus and encouraging creativity and collaboration.

They also run psychomotor circus programs designed for preschool children to enhance motor skills and coordination through playful circus activities. Spazio Circo provides specialized training for university students in Motor Sciences and Education, focusing on the educational value of circus. For secondary school students, they offer PCTO (Transversal Skills and Orientation Paths) programs that integrate circus arts into their academic journey.

In addition, Spazio Circo organizes performances and events that celebrate the joy of circus and the achievements of participants. They also run summer camps and nature-based residencies, offering immersive learning experiences in circus arts.

Inclusivity is central to their mission. The association is committed to creating accessible environments where individuals of all abilities can engage with and benefit from circus. With a multidisciplinary team of professionals in performing arts, education, psychomotricity, and motor sciences, Spazio Circo has reached thousands of students—some of whom have gone on to attend professional circus schools—demonstrating the power of circus to inspire growth, empowerment, and community.
