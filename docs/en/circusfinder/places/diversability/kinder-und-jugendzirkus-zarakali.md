# Kinder- und Jugendzirkus Zarakali

Zarakali Kinder- und Jugendzirkus is a participatory circus organization based in Frankfurt, Germany, dedicated to introducing children and teenagers to the world of contemporary circus arts. They offer training in various disciplines, including trapeze, aerial silk, unicycling, juggling, acrobatics, tightrope walking, and trampoline, catering to individuals aged 6 to 18.

Emphasizing inclusivity, Zarakali collaborates with organizations such as the Verein für Kunst und Behinderung to integrate children and youth with disabilities into their programs. This partnership ensures that participants of all abilities can experience the joy and benefits of circus arts in a supportive environment.

Beyond regular training sessions, Zarakali hosts various workshops during school holidays and weekends, and provides opportunities for performances, allowing participants to showcase their skills. Additionally, they offer their facilities for events like children's birthday parties. The organization also runs a community café in a renovated circus wagon, open on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays, fostering a sense of community among participants and their families.
