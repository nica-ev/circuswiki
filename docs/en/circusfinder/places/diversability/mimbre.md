# Mimbre

This UK-based organization runs an inclusive acrobatic and dance youth program for 6–16-year-olds, alongside a professional development program for acrobats, dancers, and physical theatre performers. They also produce and tour acrobatic and circus performances. Rather than offering disability-specific programming, they embed inclusive practices throughout all their activities. Approximately 15% of their youth participants are neurodivergent (mainly ADHD and ASD), and classes take place in wheelchair-accessible venues.

Their professional program includes two wheelchair users, and they actively collaborate with disabled artists and organizations to explore new, inclusive approaches to acrobalance. These techniques are continuously evolving through creative collaboration between disabled and non-disabled artists. While they primarily focus on acrobatics and acrobalance, they are open to adapting their methods based on the abilities and creative input of their performers.
