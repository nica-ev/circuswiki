# Centre International des Arts en Mouvement

The Centre International des Arts en Mouvement (CIAM), located in Aix-en-Provence, France, is dedicated to making circus arts accessible to individuals with disabilities through tailored workshops and collaborative projects. Throughout the year, CIAM hosts children, adolescents, and adults with various disabilities, utilizing circus as a medium for social integration and personal development. For instance, they have partnered with institutions like the Centre de Psychothérapie Infantile (CPI) in Vitrolles, offering weekly circus workshops to children aged 4 to 10 with behavioral challenges, focusing on enhancing cohesion, motor skills, and social interaction. Additionally, CIAM collaborates with the Foyer d’Accueil Médicalisé (FAM) in Bouc-Bel-Air, providing bi-weekly sessions for adults with significant disabilities to explore new activities and broaden their interests.
