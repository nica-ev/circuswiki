# BudeCirkus

BudeCirkus, based in České Budějovice, Czech Republic, is a contemporary circus organization offering courses for children and adults, including aerial and partner acrobatics. They provide workshops, performances, and tailored programs for events and team-building activities. Committed to inclusivity, BudeCirkus conducts social circus projects aimed at fostering physical skills, confidence, and teamwork among both seniors and young people.

Their "SpoluCirkus – Malej s Velkým – FamilyCirkus" program encourages family participation, promoting intergenerational bonding through circus arts. By creating a supportive environment, BudeCirkus ensures that individuals of all abilities can experience the joy and benefits of circus performance.
