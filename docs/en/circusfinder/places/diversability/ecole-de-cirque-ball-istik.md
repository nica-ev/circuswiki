# École de Cirque Ball'istik

The École de Cirque Ball'Istik is dedicated to making circus arts accessible to individuals with disabilities through its "Handicirque" program. This initiative aims to promote personal development by enhancing autonomy, expression, creativity, and social interaction. Participants engage in various circus techniques, including juggling, balancing, trapeze, and acrobatics, tailored to accommodate different abilities. The pedagogical approach emphasizes tolerance, offering diverse and open-ended activities that prioritize trust, respect, and enjoyment over performance.
