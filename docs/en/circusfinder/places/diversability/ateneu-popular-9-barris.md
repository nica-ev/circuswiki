# Ateneu Popular 9 Barris

Ateneu Nou Barris in Barcelona is a vibrant social circus group dedicated to promoting inclusion and accessibility. They offer a variety of circus arts, including juggling, acrobatics, and aerials, with the aim of fostering social inclusion, personal development, and community engagement. Their regular weekly sessions cater to diverse groups, including individuals with physical, cognitive, and emotional disabilities. The team of experienced instructors, trained through workshops and formal education, engages with a large number of participants from the local community. They also incorporate Functional Juggling methods and Juggle Board techniques to ensure inclusivity and accessibility.
