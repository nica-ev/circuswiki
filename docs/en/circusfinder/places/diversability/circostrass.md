# Circostrass

CircoStrass is an Italian social circus organization dedicated to promoting inclusion and personal development through circus arts. They offer a variety of programs and workshops tailored to individuals of all ages and abilities, focusing on disciplines such as juggling, acrobatics, and clowning. A significant aspect of their mission involves working with young people with disabilities, providing adapted circus activities that foster creativity, self-confidence, and social integration.

Their target groups include children with disabilities in primary and lower secondary schools, such as those with autism, Down syndrome, mild intellectual disabilities, and physical disabilities. They also engage adult groups associated with social associations or cooperatives. Notably, CircoStrass does not maintain a fixed group at their headquarters, emphasizing outreach and integration within existing educational and social frameworks.
