# Associazione Giocolieri E Dintorni Altro

AltroCirco, a project by Giocolieri & Dintorni, promotes social circus in Italy, uniting associations and professionals to adapt circus disciplines for young people with disabilities. A key focus is training instructors to work with diverse abilities, emphasizing the uniqueness of each individual. Activities take place in fully accessible spaces, designed for people with reduced mobility, fostering social transformation through circus arts.

The contact person responsible for programmes with people with different abilities is Tommaso Negri
