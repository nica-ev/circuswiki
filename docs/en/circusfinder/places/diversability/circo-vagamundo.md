# Circo VagaMundo

Circo VagaMundo, based in Aljezur, Portugal, is a Social and Educational Circus strongly committed to inclusion through the circus arts. The organization believes that everyone, regardless of ability, should have access to creative expression, personal development, and community connection through circus. Since 2014, Circo VagaMundo has implemented several targeted projects for young people with disabilities, working in collaboration with local and international partners.

The organization develops inclusive workshops adapted to the needs of youth with physical, intellectual, or developmental disabilities. These workshops explore circus techniques such as juggling, balancing, acrobatics, and clowning, using an approach that emphasizes individual abilities, cooperation, and fun. Circo VagaMundo has collaborated with institutions such as APPADM, Escolhas (São Teotónio), and therapeutic organizations, offering regular inclusive sessions and public performances.

By integrating disabled and non-disabled participants in their activities, Circo VagaMundo promotes empathy, self-confidence, and visibility for young people with disabilities through the joyful and empowering language of circus.
