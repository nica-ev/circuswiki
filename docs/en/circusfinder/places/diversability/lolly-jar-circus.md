# Lolly Jar Circus

Lolly Jar Circus is South Australia’s only fully inclusive social circus school, offering circus training for children and young people of all abilities. Their core mission is to use circus as a tool for social development, with a strong emphasis on inclusion, especially for young people with disabilities and those at social risk.

Participants develop physical coordination, confidence, and self-expression through activities such as juggling, acrobatics, and balance. Importantly, Lolly Jar Circus ensures that its environment is welcoming, sensory-aware, and responsive to diverse learning styles. Their trainers are specially trained in disability awareness, trauma-informed practice, and communication strategies for neurodivergent participants.

The organisation actively promotes sibling inclusion, allowing children with and without disabilities to learn and play together in the same sessions. Beyond their regular classes, they also deliver outreach workshops in special schools, disability services, and remote communities, making circus accessible to those who might otherwise be excluded.
