# National Centre for Circus Arts

The National Centre for Circus Arts is a leading UK institution offering inclusive circus programs. Their Sensory Circus initiative provides weekly classes for autistic and neurodiverse young people aged 8–11 and 12–15. Developed in collaboration with Hackney Ark and the Health Integration Team, these sessions aim to enhance confidence, coordination, and communication through circus activities. No formal diagnosis is required to participate. Additionally, the Centre offers taster workshops for schools and community groups to introduce the benefits of circus in a supportive environment.
