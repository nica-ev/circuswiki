# Rdeči Noski - Clowndoctors, društvo za pospeševanje veselja do življenja za bolne in trpeče ljudi

Rdeči noski is a Slovenian NGO and a member of Red Noses International, operating at the national level. Dedicated to bringing laughter and joy to those in need, they focus on clown visits in hospitals, elderly care facilities, and special education institutions. Their program Pester orkester includes Naša draga škatla, a performance tailored for children and adults with intellectual disabilities. Using improvisation, physical comedy, and interactive engagement, their professional clowns uplift and connect with audiences. In 2023, they performed Naša draga škatla 35 times for individuals with special needs.
