# Circoplà Scuola di Circo

Circoplà is a circus school established in 2012 in Serra de' Conti, Italy, functioning as both an Amateur Sports Association (A.S.D.) and a Social Promotion Association (A.P.S.). The organization offers a variety of courses for all ages, including acrobatics, aerial gymnastics, parkour, and circomotricità—a psychomotor development program through circus activities.

Beyond its regular classes, Circoplà engages in social circus projects aimed at promoting social inclusion and personal development. They collaborate with local entities and schools to provide educational programs tailored for individuals with diverse abilities, integrating circus arts to enhance physical coordination, creativity, and self-confidence. The organization is committed to continuous professional development, ensuring their instructors are trained in both technical skills and pedagogical approaches. Circoplà is a member of Circosfera and Altrocirco, projects of the Giocolieri e Dintorni association, and is registered in the national register of circus schools.
