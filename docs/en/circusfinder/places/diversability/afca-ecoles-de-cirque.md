# Afca Écoles De Cirque

The Association Française de Cirque Adapté (AFCA) is a French organization dedicated to promoting adapted circus arts for individuals with disabilities. AFCA offers workshops and training programs tailored to accommodate various physical and cognitive abilities, aiming to foster inclusion and personal development through circus activities. Their initiatives provide participants with opportunities to engage in creative expression, enhance motor skills, and build self-confidence within a supportive environment. By adapting traditional circus disciplines, AFCA ensures accessibility for all, encouraging social integration and celebrating diversity in the performing arts.
