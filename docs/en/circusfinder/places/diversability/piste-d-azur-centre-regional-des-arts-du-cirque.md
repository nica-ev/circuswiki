# Piste D'azur / Centre Régional des Arts du Cirque

Piste d'Azur, the Centre Régional des Arts du Cirque is dedicated to making circus arts accessible to individuals of all abilities through its "Cirque Adapté" program. This initiative focuses on adapting circus activities to meet the specific needs and goals of various institutions, including educational and therapeutic organizations. The program emphasizes personal development and group dynamics, offering a range of activities tailored to individual preferences and requirements. Sessions can be conducted under Piste d'Azur's circus tent, providing participants with an immersive experience, or at the institution's facilities, such as motor skills rooms, gymnasiums, or multipurpose halls. This flexibility allows for a customized approach that aligns with the institution's objectives and the participants' needs.
