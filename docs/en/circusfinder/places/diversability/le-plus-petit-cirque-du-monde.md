# Le Plus Petit Cirque Du Monde

Le Plus Petit Cirque du Monde (PPCM), located in Bagneux, France, is dedicated to promoting social inclusion through circus arts. Their initiatives include tailored workshops for individuals with cognitive challenges or psychological disabilities, such as autism.

These workshops focus on psychomotor development and are conducted in collaboration with healthcare professionals to ensure they meet the specific needs of each group. Sessions are typically held weekly, lasting one hour per group, with program durations ranging from ten sessions to a full year. PPCM's approach emphasizes adaptability, ensuring activities are accessible to all participants, regardless of their motor or cognitive challenges
