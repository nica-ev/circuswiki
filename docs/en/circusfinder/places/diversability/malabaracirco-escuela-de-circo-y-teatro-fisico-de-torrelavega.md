# Malabaracirco / Escuela de Circo y Teatro Físico de Torrelavega

Malabaracirco, based in Cantabria, Spain, is a professional circus company dedicated to the creation, production, and performance of circus arts. Established in 2002, the company offers a diverse range of performances, including street theater, circus shows, and workshops aimed at audiences of all ages. Their mission is to promote cultural enrichment and make circus arts accessible to the broader community.

In addition to their performances, Malabaracirco is committed to social inclusion through their educational programs. They conduct workshops and activities designed to engage individuals with disabilities, focusing on enhancing motor skills, coordination, and self-expression through circus techniques such as juggling, acrobatics, and clowning. These initiatives aim to foster creativity, boost self-esteem, and promote social integration among participants.
