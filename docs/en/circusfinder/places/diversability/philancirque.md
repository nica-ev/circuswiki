# Philancirque

Philancirque is an association based in Villeneuve-sur-Lot, France, dedicated to promoting circus arts through workshops, performances, and tailored programs. They offer discovery, initiation, and advanced workshops for individuals aged 18 months to adults, focusing on disciplines such as juggling, acrobatics, and aerial skills. The association emphasizes personal growth, creativity, and community engagement through the performing arts.

In addition to their general programs, Philancirque is committed to inclusivity by offering adapted circus workshops for individuals with disabilities, including both children and adults. These sessions can be conducted in group settings or individually, with support from specialized educators. The primary objectives are to foster social integration, combat discrimination, and provide a platform for self-expression. Participants engage in various circus disciplines, such as juggling, acrobatics, and aerial activities, through customized pathways that respect each individual's pace and abilities. Regular evaluations are conducted to monitor progress and address any challenges, ensuring a supportive and enriching experience for all involved.

Philancirque also collaborates with Medical-Educational Institutes and Therapeutic, Educational, and Pedagogical Institutes to develop and implement these specialized programs.
