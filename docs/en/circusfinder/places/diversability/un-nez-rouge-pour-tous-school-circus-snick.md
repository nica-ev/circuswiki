# Un Nez Rouge pour Tous / School Circus Snick

"Un Nez Rouge pour Tous" focuses on integrating individuals with disabilities into the performing arts, particularly through circus performances. Their productions aim to blur the lines between able-bodied performers and those with disabilities, fostering an environment where differences are embraced and celebrated. One of their notable performances, "EVOLUTION," exemplifies this mission by featuring a diverse cast that showcases the artistic potential of inclusivity. Through such initiatives, "Un Nez Rouge pour Tous" not only provides a platform for performers of all abilities but also raises awareness about the value of diversity in the arts.
