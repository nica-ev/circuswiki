# Circo Laboratorionomade

Circo Laboratorio Nomade, established in 2009 in Crispiano, Taranto, Italy, is dedicated to fostering personal growth through the fusion of sport and art. The organization offers a variety of programs encompassing activities such as flying, juggling, jumping, inverting, and climbing, all aimed at collective development.

A significant focus of their work is on social circus initiatives, utilizing circus arts as innovative tools for education and social integration. These programs are specifically designed to support children, youth, and adults with disabilities or those experiencing economic hardship, promoting inclusivity and empowerment through creative expression.
