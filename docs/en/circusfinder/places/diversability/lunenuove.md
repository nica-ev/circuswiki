# LuneNUOVE

LUNEnuove Cooperative, based at the Centro Armonico Terapeutico (CAT), has been active in social care since 2002. It focuses on education, therapy, training, and research for all ages, promoting social justice, inclusion, and well-being in harmony with nature and animals. The cooperative runs two main projects: pet therapy and educational activities for people with disabilities, as well as training courses in its holistic approach, which integrates nature, animal care, music therapy, yoga, art therapy, and more. CAT is dedicated to outdoor experiences for disabled individuals and inclusive community workshops.
