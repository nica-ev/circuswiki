# Kinder- und Jugendcircus Blamage

Circus Blamage e.V., established in 1993 in Erlenbach am Main, Germany, is a non-profit organization dedicated to fostering the motor, creative, artistic, and social skills of children and adolescents, including those with disabilities. Originating from a 1989 youth camp on the island of Pellworm, the circus has embraced inclusivity as a core principle. Their approach ensures that individuals with and without disabilities collaborate seamlessly in performances, highlighting each participant's unique talents and promoting a sense of unity. The organization hosts week-long inclusive circus camps during the Bavarian Easter, Pentecost, and summer holidays, where approximately 45 young participants train in various circus arts—such as trapeze, clowning, trampoline, pyramids, fakir acts, and juggling—culminating in public performances under their iconic red-and-white circus tent.
