# Cirk'Eole

Cirk Eole has been dedicated since 2001 to making circus arts accessible to individuals with disabilities through its "Cirque Adapté" program. This initiative serves children, adolescents, adults, and the elderly, aiming to promote personal development and social integration. The program offers weekly or biweekly sessions lasting between one to one and a half hours at the circus school, as well as discovery workshops and interventions in specialized educational settings. Each session includes a structured warm-up to prepare participants physically, followed by activities focused on developing circus techniques and fostering group cohesion through playful exercises. Ritualized components, such as consistent opening and closing routines, are incorporated to enhance group integration and mutual respect. Additionally, participants have opportunities to engage with professional circus artists in residence and attend cultural events like contemporary circus performances and the "Les Nuits d’Eole" festival.
