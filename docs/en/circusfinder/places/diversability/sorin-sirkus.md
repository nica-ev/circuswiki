# Sorin Sirkus

Sorin Sirkus, a Finnish youth circus organization, engages over 600 students weekly, supported by around 30 employees. Operating at local, national, and international levels, it promotes circus arts education and pedagogy. Since 1985, Sorin Sirkus has provided courses for all ages, including specialized programs for individuals with disabilities, at-risk youth, and families needing support. Their annual Christmas show attracts over 11,000 spectators. They teach juggling, aerial skills, balance, acrobatics, and clowning. Weekly activities include tailored programs for visually impaired individuals, children in hospitals, inmates, and young people in rehabilitation. The venue is wheelchair accessible and adapted with ramps, adjusted lighting, and audio descriptions for visually and hearing-impaired visitors.
