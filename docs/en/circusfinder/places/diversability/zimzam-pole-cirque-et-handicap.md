# ZimZam - Pôle Cirque et Handicap

The Pôle Cirque et Handicap, a collaborative initiative between the Associations La Bourguette and ZimZam, is a unique cultural space in France that integrates circus arts with disability support. Established in 2018 on the grounds of the IME La Bourguette in La Tour d’Aigues, it features a 22-meter diameter self-supporting chapiteau (circus tent) equipped with professional sound and lighting systems, accommodating up to 300 people. The facility also includes amenities such as sanitary facilities and roulottes for accommodation and dining.

The Pôle offers various programs, including circus workshops for La Bourguette beneficiaries and other regional medico-social establishments, welcoming individuals with and without disabilities. It hosts artist residencies, providing spaces for creation and interaction with the Bourguette community. The venue also organizes events like the Fadoli's Circus Festival and the Biennale Internationale des Arts du Cirque, as well as music festivals such as Watsu’live and Les Sons du Lub’. Additionally, it conducts disability awareness and training projects in collaboration with local schools and organizations.

Since its inception, the Pôle Cirque et Handicap has engaged over 400 regular circus participants, hosted 17 companies and 85 artists in residence, attracted more than 4,200 event attendees, and partnered with 15 structures in the Sud Luberon and PACA region. This initiative exemplifies a commitment to cultural accessibility and the transformative power of circus arts in fostering social inclusion.
