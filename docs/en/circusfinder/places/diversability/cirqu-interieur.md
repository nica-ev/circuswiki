# Cirqu'intérieur

Cirqu'Intérieur offers an adapted circus program specifically designed for individuals with physical, psychological, and/or mental disabilities. This initiative aims to foster personal growth by enhancing self-esteem, autonomy, confidence, and the ability to overcome personal challenges. The program utilizes tailored motor pathways and environmental adjustments to introduce various circus disciplines in a playful and progressive manner, accommodating each participant's unique abilities, needs, and aspirations. Led by Christophe Millet, who possesses over 15 years of experience in circus arts pedagogy and holds certifications including the Initiator Certificate in Circus Arts (BIAC), State Certificate in Adapted Physical and Sports Activities (APSA), and State Diploma of Specialized Educator, the program ensures a supportive and professional environment. In the previous season, Cirque Adapté engaged 101 participants across 175 hours of sessions, collaborating with various institutions such as ITEP de l’Aunis, Foyer Occupationnel Horizon, and IME Les Boucholeurs. These partnerships facilitated workshops tailored to diverse groups, including youth with behavioral challenges and adults with intellectual disabilities.
