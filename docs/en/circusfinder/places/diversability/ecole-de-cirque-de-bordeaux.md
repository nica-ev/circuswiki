# École de cirque De Bordeaux

L’École de Cirque de Bordeaux is a well-established circus school in southwestern France offering a wide range of educational and artistic programs. A key part of its mission is accessibility and inclusion, with a strong focus on adapted circus activities for young people with disabilities. The school runs inclusive workshops for children and youth with physical, sensory, intellectual, or developmental disabilities, in collaboration with specialized institutions and schools. These sessions use juggling, balance, acrobatics, aerial work, and clowning to support motor skills, creativity, and confidence-building in a safe and joyful environment. The school’s experienced instructors tailor activities to each participant’s abilities and encourage group cohesion. L’École de Cirque de Bordeaux is a member of the French Federation of Circus Schools (FFEC) and an active partner in regional and national social circus initiatives, making it a strong advocate for using circus as a tool for inclusion, empowerment, and cultural participation.
