# Associazione Corpo E Mente

This program offers circus activities for young people with disabilities, focusing on juggling and balance using the Quat Props method and Circo Sfera educational approach. It serves individuals with Parkinson’s, aphasia, ADHD, special educational needs (BES), and autism (especially Asperger’s). Structured classes run twice a week, covering aerial and ground acrobatics, balance, and juggling, with personalized sessions available. The space is fully accessible, featuring ramps and wheelchair access to ensure inclusion for all participants.
