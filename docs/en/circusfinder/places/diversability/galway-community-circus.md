# Galway Community Circus

Galway Community Circus, a non-profit organization, works with over 600 young people weekly and operates at local, national, and international levels. Their activities include youth and adult circus classes, community partnership programs, and professional circus educator training. They teach acrobatics, aerial skills, juggling, clowning, and more. The circus also provides accessible, inclusive education for schools, youth, and community groups, working with D/deaf, neurodiverse, and physically disabled young people through their Community Partnership Program. The team consists of 17 people and volunteers.
