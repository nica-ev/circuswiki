# École de cirque de loisir Supercrampe

Supercrampe, an established circus arts organization in Reims, France, has been dedicated to offering adapted circus workshops for several years. Collaborating with institutions such as the day hospital of CHU de Reims and the association 'Les Papillons Blancs', Supercrampe designs these workshops to cater specifically to individuals with disabilities. The primary goal is to create an inclusive and supportive environment where participants can explore various circus disciplines, including juggling, acrobatics, and balance exercises. These activities are tailored to enhance motor skills, boost self-confidence, and promote social interaction among attendees. Supercrampe's commitment to accessibility ensures that each session is adapted to meet the unique needs and abilities of its participants, fostering personal development and a sense of achievement.
