# La Boite à Malice ou BAM

La Boîte à Malice is a vibrant social circus school rooted in popular education and committed to inclusive creative expression. Welcoming everyone from age 2 onward, their weekly classes and seasonal workshops teach juggling, acrobatics, aerial arts, clowning, and more in a spirit of cooperation, diversity, and collective growth. Through their Cirque social initiative—part of the Projet Quartier de Cirque and Citoyen Circassien—they bring circus to youth in priority neighborhoods, using performance as a tool for empowerment and cultural inclusion. Their Cirque adapté program collaborates with specialized institutions to design adapted circus projects tailored to individual abilities, ensuring accessibility for all. They also offer circomotricité for toddlers in nurseries and animate events—from playful workshops to circus-themed performances—encouraging participation across ages and backgrounds. Grounded in values of respect, gender and social mixity, and mutual support, BàM cultivates a welcoming space where creativity, confidence, and connection flourish.
