# 4 Monkeys - asd e aps

"4 Monkeys" is an amateur sports and social promotion association founded in 2022 in Pisa, whose main aim is to spread, in their most diverse and curious forms, a passion for artistic expression and body care through physical movement. They firmly believe that the quality of daily life can be enriched through inclusive social activities of both a cultural and sporting nature.
