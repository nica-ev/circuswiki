# AirCraft Circus Academy

AirCraft Circus Academy, a member of FEDEC, offers circus classes and workshops for all ages and abilities, including specialized sessions for individuals with Special Educational Needs (SEN). They teach aerial skills (silks, hoop, trapeze), juggling, hula hoops, poi, plate spinning, and tight-wire walking. Their programs are designed for both seated and mobile individuals, including those with disabilities, dementia, and Parkinson’s. Activities can be tailored to the needs of participants, with options for group classes, one-off workshops, or private sessions.
