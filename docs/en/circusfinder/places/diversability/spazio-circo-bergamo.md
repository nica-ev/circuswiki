# Spazio Circo Bergamo

Spazio Circo Bergamo, located in Seriate, Italy, is a permanent circus centre dedicated to education, artistic creation, and community engagement through circus arts. The organization offers training programs, public performances, and educational workshops in schools. Their circus disciplines include juggling, balancing, acrobatics, and aerial acrobatics. A strong focus is placed on inclusion: the centre runs weekly adapted circus activities for young people with motor and intellectual disabilities, children with autism, and individuals affected by Alzheimer’s disease. These inclusive sessions are designed to foster motor coordination, creativity, and social interaction in a safe and playful environment. The venue is fully accessible, ensuring participation for people with reduced mobility. Spazio Circo Bergamo is committed to using circus as a tool for empowerment and expression, creating spaces where everyone, regardless of ability, can thrive and belong.
