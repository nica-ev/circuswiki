# Association Graine de Cirque

Graine de Cirque is a renowned circus school established in 1990. The school offers a diverse range of programs aimed at promoting and teaching circus arts to individuals of all ages and abilities.

A significant aspect of Graine de Cirque's mission is its commitment to inclusivity. The organization offers adapted circus workshops specifically designed for individuals with disabilities, ensuring accessibility for those with physical and mental challenges. These tailored programs aim to promote personal development, creativity, and social integration through the practice of circus arts. The facilities are equipped with ramps and accessible restrooms to accommodate participants with mobility impairments.

Graine de Cirque collaborates with schools, leisure centres, and specialized institutions to provide educational projects and interventions both on-site under their chapiteaus and off-site within various establishments. This outreach underscores their dedication to making circus arts accessible to a broad audience, including those in underserved communities.
