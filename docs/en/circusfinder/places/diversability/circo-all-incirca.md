# Circo all'inCirca

Circo all'inCirca is a contemporary circus association based in Udine, Italy, dedicated to promoting and developing circus arts through a variety of cultural activities, performances, and educational programs. They offer courses for children, teenagers, and adults, focusing on disciplines such as acrobatics, juggling, aerial skills, and balance techniques.

In collaboration with "La Nostra Famiglia," Circo all'inCirca has initiated the "Circo Adattato" project, aimed at making circus arts accessible to individuals with special educational needs and disabilities. This initiative involves studying and adapting circus disciplines and equipment to suit educational settings that include participants with intellectual and/or physical disabilities. The project focuses on creating prototypes and materials that allow individuals with various disabilities to experience the sensations associated with circus activities, such as balance, suspension, and flexibility. The ultimate goal is to foster inclusivity and personal development through tailored circus experiences.

Circo all'inCirca's commitment to inclusivity extends to organizing festivals and social projects that encourage artistic exchange among young professionals, companies, students, and enthusiasts. Their efforts contribute to a broader understanding and appreciation of contemporary circus as a tool for social inclusion and artistic expression.
