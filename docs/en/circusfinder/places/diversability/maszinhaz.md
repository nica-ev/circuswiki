# MáSzínház

MáSzínház is an inclusive performing arts community in Hungary focused on enhancing the social and creative skills of individuals, particularly those with intellectual disabilities. Through collaborative art sessions, they help develop participants' abilities and showcase these talents in public performances, promoting visibility and acceptance.

The organization conducts regular art workshops aimed at skill development and personal growth. In addition to workshops, they also present inclusive theater performances, giving participants a platform to display their talents. MáSzínház also offers educational outreach programs for schools, encouraging empathy and understanding among students.

MáSzínház is dedicated to integrating young individuals with intellectual disabilities into their programs. They use participatory theater techniques to foster social interaction, confidence, and a sense of belonging. Their mission emphasizes a society where everyone is defined by their actions rather than their limitations.
