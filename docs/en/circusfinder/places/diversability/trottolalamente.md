# TrottolaLaMente

TrottolaLaMente is a multidisciplinary center based in Barzanò, Lecco (Italy), dedicated to the care and support of adults and elderly individuals. The center focuses on prevention, rehabilitation, and personalized care for people with acquired brain injuries, cognitive decline, and neurodegenerative diseases. Their approach actively involves both the patient and their family, guiding them through tailored rehabilitation programs supported by a professional team of educators, therapists, and healthcare providers.

One of TrottolaLaMente’s key strengths is offering experiential activities that promote autonomy and well-being, such as assisted holidays in protected environments with the support of trained educators. The center also organizes cultural courses covering topics like theology, history, and literature to foster community engagement and intellectual stimulation.
