# Circo Sotto Sopra e Compagnia LegÀmi

Circo Sotto Sopra is an educational circus project based in Bologna, Italy, dedicated to promoting the art of circus among children, adolescents, and families. Established in 2017, the organization offers a variety of programs, including the Scuola di Circo Sotto Sopra, which provides courses tailored to different age groups: MiniCircus (3-5 years), Fantasiopoli (4-7 years), Sottosopra (6-10 years), and Up&Down (11-13 years). These courses focus on disciplines such as juggling, acrobatics, balance techniques, clowning, and physical theater, aiming to foster creativity, physical coordination, and social interaction among participants.

In addition to its general programs, Circo Sotto Sopra is committed to inclusivity through initiatives like "CiAU!" and "Emozioni Circensi." These projects are specifically designed for children on the autism spectrum, those with Down syndrome, and cognitive disabilities. Conducted in individual or small group settings, the programs use guided creative games and exercises to support social skills and autonomy in daily activities. Sessions are led by trained circus-theater operators, with active participation from a psychotherapist to monitor progress and maintain close communication with families and educational institutions.

Circo Sotto Sopra also extends its educational outreach to schools, offering workshops for various educational levels, and hosts Family Circus sessions where parents and children can explore circus arts together, strengthening familial bonds through shared creative experiences.
