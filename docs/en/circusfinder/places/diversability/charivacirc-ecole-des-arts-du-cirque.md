# Charivacirc - École des Arts du Cirque

Charivacirc, established in 1989 in Corbie, France, is a distinguished circus school accredited by the Fédération Française des Écoles de Cirque (FFEC). The school offers a comprehensive curriculum encompassing various circus disciplines such as acrobatics, juggling, balance techniques (including tightrope and unicycle), aerial skills (like trapeze and hoop), and clowning. These programs are designed for individuals starting from the age of two, fostering a family-friendly and inclusive environment.

A cornerstone of Charivacirc's philosophy is its commitment to accessibility and inclusion. The institution provides tailored programs for individuals with disabilities, ensuring that circus arts are accessible to all. Through partnerships with local organizations and specialized institutions, Charivacirc facilitates workshops and activities that cater to the unique needs of participants with disabilities, promoting personal development, self-confidence, and social integration.

The school's pedagogical approach emphasizes learning through play, allowing students to progress at their own pace within a supportive group setting. This methodology not only enhances physical abilities but also nurtures creativity and teamwork. Over its three-decade history, Charivacirc has become an integral part of the Corbie community, enriching the cultural landscape and exemplifying the transformative power of circus arts in fostering inclusivity and personal growth.
