# Sargantana Circ Inclusiu

Sargantana Circ Inclusiu, founded in 2022 by César García Pérez, promotes inclusion through contemporary circus. The company blends circus, theater, dance, live music, and magic to create interdisciplinary performances.

A key initiative, "Finestra Inclusiva," launched in 2014, uses social circus to engage individuals with functional diversity and youth at risk of exclusion. Participants train in clowning, acrobatics, balance, and juggling, culminating in public performances. The project has collaborated with over 20 associations, supporting people with autism, mental illness, and addiction recovery. It has received awards for its impact.

Beyond social projects, Sargantana Circ Inclusiu organizes the Valencirc Festival and provides consultancy services. Their work fosters inclusivity and artistic innovation in the circus world.
