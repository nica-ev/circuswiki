# Ton sur Ton

Ton sur Ton, based in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, is a foundation dedicated to promoting inclusive cultural practices across various artistic disciplines, including circus arts. In collaboration with the association Procap, they offer a weekly "handicirque" course designed for individuals with and without disabilities. These sessions are led by a professional circus instructor who holds a Bachelor's degree in social work, ensuring that the activities are both artistically enriching and socially supportive. The program aims to foster creativity, self-confidence, and social integration among participants. Additionally, Ton sur Ton actively engages in discussions and events focused on cultural inclusion, such as hosting debates and workshops on developing inclusive cultural labels.
