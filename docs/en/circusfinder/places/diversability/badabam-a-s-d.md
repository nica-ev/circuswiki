# BadaBam A.S.D.

This organization promotes inclusive circus programs in schools and specialized courses for young people with disabilities, collaborating with cooperatives and psychologists. It facilitates integration pathways, starting with individual or small-group lessons before transitioning participants into mainstream classes. Specializing in aerial disciplines, it adapts all circus arts to individual abilities, focusing primarily on children and adolescents to foster development, inclusion, and self-expression.
