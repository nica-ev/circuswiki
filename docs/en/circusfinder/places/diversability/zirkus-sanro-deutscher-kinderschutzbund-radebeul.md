# Zirkus Sanro / Deutscher Kinderschutzbund Radebeul

Zirkus Sanro, part of Der Kinderschutzbund Radebeul, is a volunteer-led circus project offering inclusive training for children and young people. They provide circus classes four times a week, with two sessions dedicated to choreography. Each year, participants showcase their skills in an annual performance and take part in village festivals.

The program includes a variety of disciplines such as floor and aerial acrobatics (silks, trapeze, straps), balance skills (tightrope, unicycle, rola bola, balance ball), juggling, hula hoop, trampoline, and fire arts.

Zirkus Sanro is committed to social inclusion, welcoming children and youth from disadvantaged backgrounds, those with a migration background, and individuals with physical and mental disabilities. Their circus activities promote creativity, teamwork, and self-confidence, ensuring that every participant, regardless of ability, can experience the joy and empowerment of circus arts in a supportive environment.
