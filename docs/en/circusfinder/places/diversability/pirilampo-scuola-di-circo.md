# Pirilampo - scuola di circo

Pirilampo Scuola di Circo, located in Macello, Turin, Italy, offers circus arts courses for individuals aged 4 to 99. Their programs include Adapted Circus Activities tailored for those with specific motor needs and various disabilities, as well as "family circus" sessions that encourage joint participation of children and adults. The school collaborates with local educational institutions, providing circus projects for kindergartens, primary, and secondary schools. Additionally, Pirilampo engages in Social Circus initiatives aimed at promoting social inclusion among disadvantaged families and minors, combating school dropout rates, and partnering with organizations to address issues like racism and violence. They also organize performances in circus, theater, and dance, featuring both students and guest artists, and offer training for social circus operators. Cultural and community events, such as concerts, screenings, and intergenerational celebrations, are also part of their offerings. The disciplines taught at Pirilampo encompass juggling, balancing, aerial acrobatics, handstands, and acrobalance. The association focuses on supporting minors and adolescents in disadvantaged situations, individuals with disabilities, youth facing mental health challenges, children and teenagers in contexts of educational poverty, as well as elderly individuals experiencing cognitive decline.
