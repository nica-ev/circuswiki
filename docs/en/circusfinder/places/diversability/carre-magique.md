# Carré Magique

"Un rond dans un carré – du cirque à l’IME" is a collaborative project initiated by the Institut Médico-Éducatif (IME) Jeannine Le Bouder in Minihy-Tréguier and Carré Magique, Pôle National Cirque en Bretagne, in partnership with Galapiat Cirque. Scheduled from April 7 to 27, 2025, this initiative aims to provide an immersive artistic experience for the youth of the Trégor Childhood Program, their families, and the local community. During this period, a circus tent will be set up at the IME, hosting workshops and performances led by circus artists Sébastien Wojdan, Elise Abonce Muhonen, Sylvain Briani Colin, musician Jean-Philippe Sureau, and radio project producer Anouk Edmont. Participants will engage in various circus disciplines, including acrobatics, juggling, and the wheel of death, focusing on themes such as risk-taking, self-confidence, and mutual trust.
