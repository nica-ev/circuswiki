# Cirknos

Cirknos is a circus school based in Lecce, Italy, offering annual courses for children, teenagers, and adults at the Manifatture Knos cultural center. 

Their programs encompass various circus disciplines, including juggling, stilt walking, balancing, acrobatics, and aerial skills, aiming to introduce participants to the secrets and magic of circus arts. 

Cirknos collaborates with events like the Circonauta Festival to promote circus arts among younger generations.
