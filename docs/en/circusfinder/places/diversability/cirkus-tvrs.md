# Cirkus Tværs

Cirkus Tværs is a free, community-based circus open to everyone—children, young people, and adults from all cultural, social, and physical backgrounds. Their drop-in training in juggling, acrobatics, aerials, unicycle and more creates a safe space where differences are celebrated and participants learn from one another. The focus is on cooperation, creativity, and personal growth rather than competition, making circus accessible regardless of experience or ability. Regular participants can join performances and tours, developing teamwork and confidence on stage. Cirkus Tværs also organizes inclusive public shows, community events, and workshops in schools or festivals, ensuring that circus is a tool for social inclusion, intercultural dialogue, and empowerment in the diverse Gellerup neighborhood and beyond.
