# Zirkozaurre

Zirkozaurre is a contemporary circus NGO based in Bilbao, Spain, working on both local and international levels. The organization is dedicated to the creation, training, and dissemination of circus arts through regular classes, workshops, masterclasses, and extracurricular activities. Zirkozaurre teaches a wide range of disciplines including aerial and floor acrobatics, handstands, acroportes, juggling, and diabolo.

They host artistic residencies, produce contemporary circus performances through their in-house company, and support the circus community by renting out their facilities. Zirkozaurre also engages in inclusive circus projects, often working with people with neurodevelopmental disorders. These initiatives are project-based, adapting to the needs of the specific group involved, and reflect the organization’s commitment to using circus as a tool for creativity, expression, and inclusion.
