# Circus Sonnenstich

Circus Sonnenstich, part of the Zentrum für bewegte Kunst e.V. in Berlin, is an inclusive circus project involving around 50 artists with Down syndrome, supported by 10+ trainers. They offer regular training, performances, and educational programs, promoting inclusive circus pedagogy. Training includes acrobatics, dance, trapeze, balance techniques (Rola Bola, walking globe, stilts), diabolo, juggling, and hula-hoop. Their primary target group is individuals with Down syndrome. Activities are held regularly, with artists grouped by skill levels for continuous development.
