# Les Fortiches École de Cirque de Sommières

Les Fortiches, a circus school located in Sommières, France, offers a "Cirque Adapté" program designed to serve as an educational and therapeutic tool for individuals with physical or mental disabilities. This initiative focuses on personal and collective experimentation, aiming to empower participants through the diverse disciplines of circus arts. Each project is developed in collaboration with Les Fortiches' pedagogical team, ensuring a tailored approach that prioritizes individual progress over technical performance. This methodology includes specific evaluations to monitor and support the evolution of each participant.
