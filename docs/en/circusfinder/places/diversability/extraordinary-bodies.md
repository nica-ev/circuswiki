# Extraordinary Bodies

Extraordinary Bodies, a collaborative partnership between Cirque Bijou and Diverse City, is a national-level inclusive performance company in the UK. They create and tour circus performances with artists both with and without disabilities. The company also offers workshops, training, and community initiatives to promote inclusivity in the performing arts. They utilize aerial arts, acrobatics, juggling, and physical theatre, with a focus on accessibility and adaptability. Activities are project-based and include regular workshops, training, rehearsals, and community engagement.
