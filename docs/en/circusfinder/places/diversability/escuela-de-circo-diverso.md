# Escuela de Circo Diverso

Circo Diverso, located in Tres Cantos, Madrid, is a circus school founded in 2007 by the local youth association to address the city's cultural needs. The school offers training in various circus disciplines, including juggling, acrobatics, aerial techniques, and clowning, catering to participants of all ages and skill levels. A core aspect of Circo Diverso's mission is inclusivity, particularly integrating individuals with functional diversity. To this end, they allocate 20% of their program spots to people with disabilities, ensuring accessibility and promoting social integration through circus arts. The school operates in municipal facilities, notably the Polideportivo de La Luz, and has expanded its reach by offering courses at the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid since 2014. Additionally, Circo Diverso organizes the annual CircAlle Festival, showcasing various circus companies and fostering community engagement.
