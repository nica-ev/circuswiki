# Drustvo CIK

Association CIK is a Slovenian NGO focused on youth work and education through circus pedagogy and social games. It trains youth workers, equipping them with creative methods to engage youngsters while promoting mental well-being. Circus pedagogy blends contemporary circus, theatre, and games to foster teamwork, trust, and resilience. Social games encourage self-awareness and address social issues. CIK also promotes mental hygiene, international mobility, and European collaboration. It conducts workshops, performances, and professional counselling, aiming to develop circus pedagogy in Slovenia and abroad.
