# Scuola di Circo Corsaro

Scuola di Circo Corsaro is a social circus project founded in 2006 by Maria Teresa Cesaroni in Scampia, a suburb in the north of Naples, Italy. This area is known for its socio-economic challenges, including high unemployment rates and significant presence of organized crime. The school aims to engage children and teenagers from both the local community and nearby Romani camps, offering them training in various circus disciplines such as juggling, acrobatics, balancing, clowning, and aerial acrobatics on trapeze and fabrics. By participating in these activities, students learn the importance of teamwork, discipline, and mutual respect. The program serves as a tool for social integration, helping to build self-esteem and provide a constructive alternative to the adverse influences prevalent in their environment. Over the years, Scuola di Circo Corsaro has collaborated with other circus and theater professionals and has participated in festivals like Circomondo in Siena. Currently, the school's activities are conducted in the Municipal Gym of Piscinola, continuing its mission to empower youth through the art of circus.
