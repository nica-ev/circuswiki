# Občianske združenie ČERVENÝ NOS Clowndoctors

Červený Nos Clowndoctors is a Slovak non-governmental organization dedicated to bringing joy and laughter to those in need, particularly children in hospitals and elderly individuals in care facilities. Their team of professional clowns conducts regular visits, performances, and specialized programs aimed at enhancing the psychosocial well-being of patients. While their primary focus is on pediatric and geriatric populations, they also engage with individuals with special needs through tailored performances and interactive sessions. Their approach utilizes the art of clowning, incorporating improvisation, physical comedy, and interactive performances to create uplifting experiences for their audiences.
