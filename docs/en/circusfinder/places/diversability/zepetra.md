# Zepetra

Zepetra – École de Cirque is an inclusive circus school offering weekly classes and workshops for all ages and abilities. Their adapted circus program, based on AFCA methodology, uses circus arts as an educational and therapeutic tool. Since 2005, Zepetra has partnered with medico-social institutions and sensory-impaired facilities to provide tailored workshops that develop motor skills, personal growth, and social interaction. Activities are designed in collaboration with professionals to support each participant’s development. Zepetra also hosts inclusive public events, performances, and open workshops, promoting social and cultural inclusion through circus arts.
