## Circus School Or Juggling Workshop

Circo Diverso is a non-profit circus association based in Tres Cantos (Madrid, Spain), founded in 2007. We promote contemporary circus as a tool for education, inclusion, creativity and community building. Our activities include regular classes for children, teenagers and adults, workshops, social circus projects, international exchanges, performances, festivals and cultural events. We welcome everyone, from complete beginners to experienced circus artists, and actively collaborate with local communities, schools, youth organisations and international circus networks. Our goal is to make circus accessible to all while fostering diversity, participation and artistic development.
