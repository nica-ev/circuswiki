# Eastside Spin Jam

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

Join us every SUNDAY for Eastside Spin Jam!
Eastside Spin Jam is open to flow artists, jugglers, and any other person who wants to move and flow.
All props and skill levels are welcome and encouraged.
See you Sunday!!
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**What should I expect?
Friendly faces and welcoming spaces. Open jams are an unstructured time to come together and practice flow arts while building connections and community. Many love teaching. All props, skill levels and movement modalities are encouraged and welcome!
We plan to host workshops on a monthly basis. Let the event host know if you're interested in teaching!
**Don't have a prop of your own? We've always got extra to share.
