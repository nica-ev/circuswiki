## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

Fabrek hosts, weekly, monthly and yearly gatherings, from small flow and juggle jams to large scale circus festivals all over Lebanon.

Fabrek is a social enterprise that aims to connect people of all backgrounds, ages and social groups through Circus, movement and urban arts.

"Our goal is to spread awareness over the art of Circus, through all circus entities and groups in Lebanon"
