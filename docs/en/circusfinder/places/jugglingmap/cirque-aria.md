## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

Weekly juggling meetup at Cirque Aria on the west side of San Antonio. Indoors, high ceilings, and AC! Lots of props to try, all experience levels welcome. Come practice with fellow jugglers or come on by and learn to juggle.
