# Portland Jugglers Weekly Juggling

Portland Jugglers Weekly Juggling is listed on the community-maintained JugglingMap as a juggling training or juggling club.

Location as provided by the source: Reed College Sports Center, Southeast Botsford Drive, Portland, Oregon, United States

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

Weekly juggling club that started when Reed College began offering juggling as a PE class in 1979, and it was open to the public who frequently brought new skills to the college class. The public component grew into the Portland Jugglers. Most weeks we host a number of 30-minute workshops for the PE students and others. Juggling takes place in the gym during the school year and out on the lawn during summer break. Please join our mailing list at https://heylo.group/portlandjugglers to stay informed about weekly juggling and other Portland Jugglers events.

-------Things to Know Before You Go--------

FOOTWEAR: Clean footwear is required. You may wear outdoor shoes however they must be clean and non-marking. Please double check them to make sure there is no gravel wedged in the treads. Indoor shoes, socks, and bare feet are also welcome.

NO EXPOSED METAL PARTS: juggling clubs must not have exposed screws (even recessed screws). Felt pads on screws are not enough to prevent floor damage. Please replace club knobs that have exposed screws with Rob's Knobs, available in the gym for free ($1 suggested donation), or borrow clubs from another friendly juggler.

NO FOOD OR DRINK in the gym. Water bottles with lids are okay.
