## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

Circus Arts Flow Circus Saturdays explores the intersection between creative body movement, neuroscience and community. Juggling through the lens of neuroscience vs performance focuses on how juggling makes you feel vs what it looks like to someone else. Exercises start with ZERO barrier to entry, low stakes and high reward. You maximize neuroplasticity by allowing mistakes to rewire your brain, and you develop tools to deal with stress and trauma. Neuro-juggling is a sharable super power where difficulty and engagement can be increased through an infinite number of patterns. Saturdays 3-6pm Washington Square Park San Francisco from 4th Saturday in April thru Thanksgiving when we go on winter break. Were out unless there is rain, a holiday or neighborhood festival. Visit our website and socials to learn about monthly neuroscience themes and see our RECAP videos.
