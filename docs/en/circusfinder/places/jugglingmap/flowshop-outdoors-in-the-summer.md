# Flowshop (outdoors in the summer)

Flowshop (outdoors in the summer) is listed on the community-maintained JugglingMap as a juggling training or juggling club.

Location as provided by the source: Dahlia garden, Seattle, Washington, United States

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

(Not meeting here again until May 2027! See the related Flowshop indoors entry for Winter 2026)

Join us every Tuesday for FlowShop! We meet in the grassy area near the conservatory and the dahlia garden (both show up in google maps!).


FlowShop is a weekly gathering for flow artists, jugglers, dancers, and other movement artists to come together for practice and play. Our events are open to people of all backgrounds, skills, and levels of experience.


Interested in teaching a workshop? Fill out our application here: https://forms.gle/jDiSjYLTsEAHubhP8

------------------------------------------------------------------------
**How To Find Us at Volunteer Park: Use GPS to navigate to the Volunteer Park Dahlia Garden. You'll be able to see the Volunteer Park Conservatory at the end of the road (It's a white building with lots of windows). When the conservatory is at your back, you'll see us on the grassy hill below.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
**What should I expect at FlowShop?

Friendly faces and welcoming spaces. Open jams are an unstructured time to come together and practice flow arts while building connections and community. Many love teaching! All props, skill levels and movement modalities are encouraged and welcome!

Don't have a prop of your own? We've always got extra to share.
