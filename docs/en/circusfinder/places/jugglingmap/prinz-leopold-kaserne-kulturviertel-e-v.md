## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

We have once in a Month here our Firejam from the Regensburg Community it's usually at the first Wednesday. It's out side or when it's raining under a half open old construction Building.
We have a Dipping station and a Toolholder too here but you must bring your own oil. Musicans where also welcome to jam here.
You can get Drinks an Donation Basis in the kitchen.
