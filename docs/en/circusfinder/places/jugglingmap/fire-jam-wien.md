# Fire Jam Wien

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

A loosely organised community of flow and fire artists gathering every thursday to jam together.
We gather on Donauinsel starting around 16:30 and move over to Kaisermühlenbucht when the sun sets and it gets dark.
Bring your own fuel and props, or ask others if they can lend you some.
Everyone is welcome, regardless of skill level, origin or background.
