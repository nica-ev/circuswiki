# Let's Flow! Newcastle weekly jam

Let's Flow! Newcastle weekly jam is listed on the community-maintained JugglingMap as a juggling training or juggling club.

Location as provided by the source: Foreshore Park, Newcastle, New South Wales, Australia

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

• Mondays | 5:30-7:30pm
• Train Sheds, Foreshore Park (Wharf Rd)

Come join this little rhythm with us!
Every Monday we gather to spin, flow, improvise, learn, connect and deepen our community.

Feel safe to stumble, fumble and drop your prop while learning new tricks. All skill levels are welcome, from curious beginners to experienced performers.
- Music provided
- No experience needed
- Family friendly
- Bring your props + spare props to share is encouraged
- Pack yourself a picnic dinner or snacks

Come as you are and move your body. Bring your mates, your kids, and your curiosity!

Fire Jam often follows our Flow Jam from 7:30pm. Check the weekly updates on our Facebook event to confirm fire.

Schedule supplied by the source: Mon 17:30–19:30
