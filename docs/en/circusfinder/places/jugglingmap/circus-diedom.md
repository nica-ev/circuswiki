# Circus Diedom

Circus Diedom is listed on the community-maintained JugglingMap as a circus school or juggling workshop.

Location as provided by the source: Beton-T, 4, Koningin Wilhelminalaan, Utrecht, Utrecht, Nederland

## Circus School Or Juggling Workshop

Diedom is next to Beton T. Entrence is at Koningin  Wilhelminalaan 4c.

The circus is equipped for the 5 main disciplines: Object Manipulation, Balance, Aerial, Acrobatics and Clowning.

It has high ceilings 10meters. So many Aerialist train their discipline here.

Every Tuesday morning and Thursday evening theirs open training. It's 5.- per training for non members.

Check the website of Instagram for more info
