# Odskocznia open workshops

Odskocznia open workshops is listed on the community-maintained JugglingMap as a juggling training or juggling club.

Location as provided by the source: Kosmosu, Warszawa, województwo mazowieckie, Polska

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

Odskocznia is a coaching group specialised in functional juggling and flow arts. Each summer they run open workshops as well as paid courses all over Warsaw. Currently their summer training takes place in Ogrody Kosmosu park in Włochy district. Basic props and circus-related equipment is provided by the coaches, though you are more than welcome to join with your own gear. This year the number of participants reached 60 people at once. The schedule changes every year so make sure to visit their social media to see how you can meet them.

Access: free
