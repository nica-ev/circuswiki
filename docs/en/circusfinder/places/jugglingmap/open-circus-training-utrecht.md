# Open circus training Utrecht

Open circus training Utrecht is listed on the community-maintained JugglingMap as a juggling training or juggling club.

Location as provided by the source: 2A, Deken Roesstraat, Utrecht, Utrecht, Nederland

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

Training and hangout spot for all circus disciplines. We rent the hall every year from September until the NJF somewhere in May. After NJF we irregularly train outside in the Wilhelminapark om Thursday evenings.

In the hall there are some rigging points for aerials but you have to bring your own silks/rope/hoop/etc.

Schedule supplied by the source: Thu 20:00–22:00

Access: constrained: 5 euro per session
