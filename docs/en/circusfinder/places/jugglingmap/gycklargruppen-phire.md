Gycklargruppen Phire is listed on the community-maintained JugglingMap as a juggling training or juggling club.

Location as provided by the source: Luleå tekniska universitet, Laboratorievägen, Luleå, Sverige

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

Circus and juggling club Phire arrange skill-share style practise three times a week at different locations around LTU (Luleå University of Technology). Mondays and Fridays are in gym hall with possibility for all disciplines including aerial, Tuesdays in a big classroom with high celining but no rigging points. Contacts us before coming by by email, because some of the doors are locked in the evening and you need help to get in of you are a visitor! Welcome!

Schedule supplied by the source: Mon 18:00–19:30, Tue 16:30–18:30, Fri 17:00–19:00

Access: constrained: Free for visitors and the first three times, then 100 SEK/half a year to become a member with unlimited practise that half-year.
