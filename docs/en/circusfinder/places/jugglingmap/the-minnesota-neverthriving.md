# The Minnesota Neverthriving

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

The Minnesota Neverthriving is a juggling club that meets at Matthews Park on 29th Ave and 24th St in south Minneapolis. All are welcome. Bring your own props! (or come without props, we have lots! Beginners welcome!)
Indoor Club
Mondays  6 PM - 8 PM Inside the Rec center
Saturdays 2 PM - 4 PM Inside the Rec center
Indoor club is inside the Matthews Park rec center. We are usually in the Multi Purpose room to the left after waking in, but can sometimes be relocated to the large gym on the right.
In nice weather, we can often be found outdoors between the basketball court and the Volleyball court.
Outdoor Club (weather permitting usually May-October)
Mondays 6 PM to dark
Saturdays 2 PM - 4 PM
Our outdoor spot has shade. Bug spray recommended, but we always have it to share.
