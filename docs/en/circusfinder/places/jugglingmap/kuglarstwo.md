## Juggling Shop

Kuglarstwo is a juggling + flow arts + magic store and brand. They manufacture fireshow props available in many parts of the world.
The store is open from monday to friday, 10 am to 6 pm. It's 2 metro stations away from the city center and main train station.
On tuesday evenings there are often magic meetings, where a bunch of geeky guys are learning and sharing card tricks, eating pizza and, on occasion, giving presentations about their profession as illusionists.
If you want to learn about shows and training in Warsaw, this is a good place to ask. The store's website has an entire calendar dedicated to circus-related events.
