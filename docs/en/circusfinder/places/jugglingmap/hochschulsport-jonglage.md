## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

Hi! We are a group of  jugglers from beginners to semi-professional level. Most of us are toss jugglers - so if you are looking for a passing partner (or want to learn it) come and join us! Currently, some of us are also doing diabolo, bounce, poi and contact... If you don't have your own props, we can provide some equipment.
