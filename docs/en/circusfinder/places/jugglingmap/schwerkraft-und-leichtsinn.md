# Schwerkraft und Leichtsinn

Schwerkraft und Leichtsinn is listed on the community-maintained JugglingMap as a juggling training or juggling club.

Location as provided by the source: Lemgo, Nordrhein-Westfalen, Deutschland

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

Open Meeting in a very large sportshall.
Juggling meetup for all skill levels. Complete beginners are also very welcome. We juggle and love club passing. In addition, unicycling, partner acrobatics, trapeze, and more are also options.
The meetup is organized by the TV Lemgo, but everyone is invited to just drop by and check it out.
In the summer, when the weather is nice, we sometimes practice on the Lemgo city wall (behind the restaurant Zündkerze).

Schedule supplied by the source: Sun 18:00–20:00
