# Atlanta Jugglers Association

Atlanta Jugglers Association is listed on the community-maintained JugglingMap as a juggling training or juggling club.

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

We meet outside on the lawn in front of Little Five Points Community Center. During winter months or if it's raining, windy or too hot we are in our room 202 on the third floor. There is an elevator off the back parking lot and plenty of free accessible parking. We eat together afterwards at a nearby restaurant.

Home of the annual Groundhog Day Jugglers Festival on the first weekend in February.
