# Bryant Park Jugglers

Bryant Park Jugglers is listed on the community-maintained JugglingMap as a juggling training or juggling club.

Location as provided by the source: Bryant Park, New York, NY, United States

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

Jugglers juggling. We teach, too!
Juggling props are provided free to use at the park during the designated juggling times, just bring yourself.

New Location as of April 2026:
Primary location: On the shuffleboard court, SW corner of Bryant Park (6th Ave & 40th)
Secondary locations: Terrace behind the library and the Bryant Park lawn
*The group has been known to wander to different spots in Bryant Park. If you don't see anyone, take a walk around the park/library and look for the flying clubs.

Saturday meetups are only April-December. all other meetups are year round.

Schedule supplied by the source: Mon 12:00–13:00, Tue 12:00–13:00, Tue 17:30–19:30, Wed 12:00–13:00, Thu 12:00–13:00, Fri 12:00–13:00, Sat 11:00–14:00
