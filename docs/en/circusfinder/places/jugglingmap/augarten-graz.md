## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

During the warm season you can meet jugglers any day at any time, but most probably in the afternoon/evening. We meet next to the skating halfpipe. If there is someone of the community you will see objects in the air. Mixed group of jugglers and spinners. During school times we meet preferably on friday late afternoon.
