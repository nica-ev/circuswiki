## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

An informal juggling group based in central Wellington. We meet most Wednesdays 6:30 - 8:30 at Te Whaea, the National Dance and Drama Centre on Hutchison Road, Newtown. Cost: Koha
We then throw stuff at each other. After that you'll invariably find us hanging out at Heaven on Cuba St.
Like most jugglers, we share toys ... you don't need props to come! And we sometimes have biscuits ... yum ...
