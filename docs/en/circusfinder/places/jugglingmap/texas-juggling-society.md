# Texas Juggling Society

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

We meet every Thursday all year long from 7 pm - 10 pm. We meet in Gregg Hall. (This is the building behind the church.) Texas Juggling Society meetings are free of charge and open to jugglers, hoopers, poi swingers, diaboloists, yoyo throwers, kendama players, and circus art folks of all abilities. Beginners and spectators are welcome. Yes, that means you! Come on down!

** We have a QR code inside the building to scan and enter your license plate number to park free! **
