## Juggling Shop

Fire artists making props for fire artists

I'm a fire performer first. For years I travelled, performed and trained, constantly looking for better props. Most of what was available didn't match what I needed, so I started building my own. In 2018 I began making props for other performers while on the road, and that obsession became Juggling Calling. Today we're a team of 5, producing everything from scratch, sourcing, building and customising each prop ourselves. The same mindset that got me started still drives everything: make equipment that performers actually want to use, without turning into a souless marketing machine. Here you'll find: • Props built from materials we've tested and selected over many years • Designs shaped by a decade of prototyping, observing and performing with fire props • Custom options so your props fit your style • Components and materials if you prefer to build your own I approach this work as both a craftsman and an artist. Fire and flow arts are still young, and there's a lot left to explore in materials, prop design, and artistic direction. If you care about how your equipment feels, how long it lasts, and the details that make a prop genuinely satisfying to use, you're in the right place. (Most) Orders are prepared and shipped within 3 working days.
— Guillaume
