# Zurich Juggling Club at Rote Fabrik

Zurich Juggling Club at Rote Fabrik is listed on the community-maintained JugglingMap as a juggling training or juggling club.

Location as provided by the source: Bewegigs Ruum, Seestrasse, Zürich, Zürich, Schweiz/Suisse/Svizzera/Svizra

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

Where: Rote Fabrik (Wollishofen). Main entrance to site. Enter building thought 2nd door on the left. 1st floor on your right hand.
If the door downstairs should be closed, place yourself in the entrance underneath the window and whistle, scream, throw a pebble on the window, etc.
We are in the corner space (street/entrance) on the 1st floor.
Summertime + nice weather: at the lake at Savera-Areal (near GZ Wollishofen + near Schiffstation).

Schedule supplied by the source: Mon 20:00–22:00

Access: constrained: If you just visit, it's free. If you attend regularly, you need an annual membership of IG Rote Fabrik.
