# The Easton Sideshow

The Easton Sideshow is listed on the community-maintained JugglingMap as a juggling training or juggling club.

Location as provided by the source: Larry Holmes Statue, Two Rivers Trailway - Easton, Easton, Pennsylvania, United States

## Juggling Training Or Juggling Club

The meetups aren't regular.  They may be daily, they may be weekly, they may start as 2 people just waking up and end with 7 performing at a crowded party.  It may be a casual day of a few jugglers at the park or it might be a night filled with glow juggling, dancing, and bar hopping.  The Easton Sideshow is whatever we (and you) want it to be.  Send a message in the group of when you want to meetup or just say "heading downtown who wants to juggle" and chances are you will find others.

Access: free
