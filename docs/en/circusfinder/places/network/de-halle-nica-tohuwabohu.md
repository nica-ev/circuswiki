# NICA e.V. / Tohuwabohu Halle e.V.

NICA e.V. is a circus network and organization based in Halle (Saale). It develops inclusive circus methods, connects practitioners and organizations, and provides resources for circus pedagogy. Tohuwabohu Halle e.V. is the associated local organization behind practical circus activities and the Tohuwabohu convention.

The marker shows the office address published on NICA's official website: Thüringer Straße 30, Haus 1, Eingang 2, Raum 206.
