> [!info] Short Description
> Pairs race while holding three clubs between their bodies without using their hands.

**Group Size**: 2 to 60 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Three clubs per pair, race course  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Players work in pairs. They press one club between their chests, one between their shoulders and one between their foreheads, then race to the finish without dropping the clubs. The fastest pair with all three clubs still in place wins.

## Variations

Use only one or two clubs for beginners. Add a turn-around point for a longer race.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
