> [!info] Short Description
> Players balance an agreed object, such as a club or even a unicycle, on the chin or another defined balance point.

![[../img/games-club-balance-guiness.jpg|300]]

**Group Size**: 3 to 60 players  
**Difficulty**: schwer  
**Material**: Clubs, unicycles or other balance objects  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Players balance an agreed object, such as a club or even a unicycle, on the chin or another defined balance point. The last player still balancing wins.

## Variations

Add movement or a small obstacle course if the basic balance is too easy.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
