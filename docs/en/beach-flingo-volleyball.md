> [!info] Short Description
> Teams play a volleyball-like game using Beach Flingo bibs or throw-catch cloths and a ball.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: schwer  
**Material**: Beach Flingo bibs or throw-catch cloths, ball, net or court line  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Teams play a volleyball-like game using Beach Flingo bibs or throw-catch cloths and a ball. The aim is to send the ball over the net and make the other side miss the catch or return.

## Variations

Use the same scoring as simple volleyball.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
