> [!info] Brief Description
> A team game where groups retrieve materials from the center and from each other to be the first to complete announced tasks.

**Group Size**: 16-32 people
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: Hoops and various small equipment
**Game Duration**: 10-15 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A game for medium to large groups.

Game variant for 16 people:
Distribute 4 hoops around the room (as far apart as possible), and place 1 hoop in the center. Place 4 different types of materials in the center hoop, with 4 of each item (e.g., 4 balls, 4 scarves, 4 sticks, and 4 rings).
Divide the group into 4 teams of 4 people each, positioning them at the empty hoops.
The game leader announces a task, for example: "4 identical items," "4 different items," or "empty hoop."
The teams must then try to retrieve the necessary materials from the center hoop. However, only one player from each team may go at a time, alternating turns.
The goal of the game is to be the first to complete the task set by the game leader. Teams can hinder each other by taking items from other teams' hoops or by placing items into them.
