> [!info] Short Description
> A relay course combines several circus skills.

**Group Size**: 6 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Mixed circus props, course markers  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

A relay course combines several circus skills. One player completes the first task, which triggers the next player, and so on until the team reaches the finish. Tasks can include balloon popping, devil stick movement, diabolo balance, juggling and unicycling.

## Variations

Build the course from the skills your group already knows.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
