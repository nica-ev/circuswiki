> [!info] Short Description
> A calm circle game where clapping impulses are passed on and tracked through eye contact.

**Group Size**: 5+ people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A clapping game for any group size.

Everyone stands in a circle.
The group leader starts by passing a clap to any person in the circle through eye contact. That person then makes eye contact with someone else and passes the clap on to them...
The leader can introduce any number of additional claps into the circle. When a clap returns to the person who initiated it, they do not pass it on again.
The game ends when all claps have returned to the leader.
