> [!info] Short Description
> Players keep a devil-stick propellor going while playing a gladiators-style knockout round.

**Group Size**: 3 to 60 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Devil sticks and handsticks  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Players keep a devil-stick propellor going while playing a gladiators-style knockout round. They may be required to use one hand, switch hands, or change propellor direction during the game. A player is out after losing the propellor or breaking the agreed rule.

## Variations

Use both handsticks for a more accessible version, or restrict direction changes for advanced players.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
