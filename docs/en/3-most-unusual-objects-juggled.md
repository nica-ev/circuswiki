> [!info] Short Description
> Participants present unusual objects that they can juggle.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: einfach  
**Material**: Unusual objects chosen by participants  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Participants present unusual objects that they can juggle. The audience votes for the most surprising or entertaining choice. If there are many entrants, drops can be used for elimination.

## Variations

Avoid food, liquids or messy objects unless cleanup is planned.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
