> [!info] Short Description
> Poi spinners begin with a cross-and-follow pattern.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Poi, bucket  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Poi spinners begin with a cross-and-follow pattern. On the count, they release one poi and try to land it in a bucket in the middle of the arena. A clean bucket shot scores or advances.

## Variations

Increase distance or use a smaller bucket for later rounds.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
