> [!info] Short Description
> A diabolo throwing contest where the highest clean throw and catch wins.

![[../img/diabolo-highest-throw.jpg|300]]

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: One diabolo and handsticks per player, open high space  
**Duration**: approx. 5-10 minutes

## Game Description

All players throw their diabolos high at the same time and must catch cleanly to stay in the game. A player is out if the throw is unsafe, uncatchable or not caught cleanly.

Repeat rounds until only a few players remain. In the final, the winner is the player who makes the highest safe throw and still catches it.

## Setup

- Use a high indoor hall or outdoor space with no overhead obstacles.
- Mark a throwing area and keep spectators well outside the landing zone.
- Decide whether players must catch their own diabolo or may catch any diabolo.

## Variations

- Allow catching any diabolo for chaotic convention play.
- Require each player to catch their own diabolo for a stricter version.
- Use distance-to-self instead of height.
- Allow partner catches for a team version.

## Safety Notes

High diabolos are hard and unpredictable. Clear the landing area and stop if throws drift toward spectators.
