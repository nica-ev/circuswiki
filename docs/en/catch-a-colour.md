> [!info] Short Description
> Players throw their diabolos into the air.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Diabolos in several colors  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Players throw their diabolos into the air. While they are airborne, the caller announces a color. Players try to catch a diabolo of that color; successful players continue to the next round.

## Variations

Use colored tape on diabolos if the group does not have enough different colors.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
