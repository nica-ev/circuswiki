> [!info] Brief Description
> A circle game where random eye contact causes two people to freeze until only a few remain.

**Group Size**: 5-25 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A circle game for small to medium-sized groups.

Everyone stands in a circle, looking down at the floor.
The game leader counts "1-2-3," then everyone lifts their heads and makes eye contact with a person of their choice.
If two people's gazes meet by chance, they "freeze" each other and fall to the ground with a theatrical gesture.
The last two people still standing in the circle win.
