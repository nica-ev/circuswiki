> [!info] Brief Description
> A group task game where participants find body contact points and group sizes that match given prompts.

**Group Size**: 4-30 people
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: 5-15 minutes

## **Game Description**:

This game is suitable for any group size.

Everyone walks around the room.

The game leader announces a task, for example, "4 people, 3 heads, 6 legs."

Now, participants must quickly form groups of four. They need to place their heads on the floor and also touch the floor with their feet. However, two people can only stand on one leg each to fulfill the "6 legs" requirement.

The next task is: "2 people, 2 legs, 4 hands, 2 noses."

Now, pairs of two must be formed. Both individuals must stand on one leg, placing their hands and noses on the floor.

...

The tasks can start simply and increase in complexity from round to round.

The game can end when a task can no longer be completed by everyone.
