> [!info] Short Description
> Players arrange juggling clubs into a hat-like structure on their head.

**Group Size**: 3 to 60 players  
**Difficulty**: schwer  
**Material**: Juggling clubs  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Players arrange juggling clubs into a hat-like structure on their head. The aim is to knock off other players' club hats while keeping your own in place. The last player still wearing a club hat wins.

## Variations

Use lightweight clubs and forbid hits to the face or head.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
