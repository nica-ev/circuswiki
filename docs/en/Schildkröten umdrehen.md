> [!info] Brief Description
> A physical movement game where players lying on their stomachs, acting as turtles, try not to be flipped over.

**Group Size**: 6 to 30 participants
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: 5 to 10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Depending on the players' strength and size, one or two "turtle flippers" are designated. All other players lie down on their stomachs and become turtles. They can move around, but only while lying down! The turtle flipper(s) try to flip the turtles onto their backs. As soon as a turtle's back touches the ground, they also become a turtle flipper. The turtles, of course, don't want this to happen and therefore try to hold onto each other or develop other tactics to avoid being flipped over. The game ends when there are no more turtles left.
