> [!info] Brief Description
> A circle game where participants protect themselves from the chaser in the center by forming pairs.

**Group Size**: 8+ people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: 4-9 minutes

## **Game Description**:

All players stand in a circle. One person is designated as the chaser and stands in the center of the circle. The chaser can then try to tag players standing in the circle. However, players can protect themselves by forming pairs. If three players stand together, the chaser can choose one of the three to tag. If two players form a pair and the chaser cannot tag them, the chaser can spin around once within the circle. At this moment, the pair must separate, and the two players must quickly form a pair with another neighbor. Otherwise, they can be tagged. If someone is tagged, they must go into the circle, and the chaser can take their former position.
With larger groups, multiple players can stand in the circle and try to tag the outer players.
