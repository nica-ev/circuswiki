> [!info] Short Description
> Two people hold a staff or bar while players pass underneath it juggling their chosen prop.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Juggling props, staff or limbo bar  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Two people hold a staff or bar while players pass underneath it juggling their chosen prop. After each round the bar is lowered. The last player to pass under cleanly while still juggling wins.

## Variations

Allow different props, or run separate rounds for balls, clubs and diabolo.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
