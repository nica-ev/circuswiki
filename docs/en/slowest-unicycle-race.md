> [!info] Short Description
> Riders cross a lane as slowly as possible without idling, stopping or leaving the course.

**Group Size**: 3 to 60 players  
**Difficulty**: schwer  
**Material**: Unicycles, lane markers  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Riders cross a lane as slowly as possible without idling, stopping or leaving the course. The last legal rider to cross the finish line wins.

## Variations

Use narrow lanes for advanced riders and wider lanes for beginners.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
