> [!info] Short Description
> A mixed-prop gladiators game where everyone chooses a prop they can keep active safely.

**Group Size**: 3 to 60 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Any circus prop that can be kept active safely  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Everyone chooses a prop they can keep active, such as clubs, plate, diabolo, devil stick or another circus object. Players enter the arena together and try to make others lose control while keeping their own prop going.

## Variations

Good for mixed-level groups because each participant can choose an appropriate prop.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
