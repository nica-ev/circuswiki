> [!info] Brief Description
> A name game where names are repeated and remembered with a matching adjective and animal or profession.

**Group Size**: 4 to 12 participants
**Difficulty**: easy
**Materials**: none
**Playing Time**: 3-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

This game is ideal for groups who don't know each other at all. One player starts by saying their name, an adjective, and an animal or profession. The adjective and the animal or profession must start with the same letter as the player's name. For example: Robert the running robot. Now it's the turn of the player to the first person's right. This player repeats what the first person said and then comes up with something suitable for their own name. The third player repeats what the first and second players said and adds their own phrase. This continues until everyone has had a turn. In the end, the first player must repeat everything that was said.
