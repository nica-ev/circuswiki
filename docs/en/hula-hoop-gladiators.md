> [!info] Short Description
> A hula-hoop knockout game based on the gladiators convention-game format.

![[../img/hula-hoop-gladiators.jpg|300]]

**Group Size**: 4 to 40 players
**Difficulty**: mittel
**Material**: One hula hoop per player, marked playing area
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Players keep a hoop active while moving inside an arena. They try to make other players lose control of their hoop without unsafe contact.

## Setup

- Give every player a hoop.
- Define what counts as active: waist hooping, hand hooping, or carrying the hoop in a specific position.
- Mark the arena and agree on allowed interference.

## Rules

1. All players start the agreed hoop action at the same time.
2. Players move through the arena and try to disturb other players' hoop action using only the allowed contact.
3. A player is out when their hoop drops, stops, leaves the field, or they use illegal contact.
4. Eliminated players leave the arena with their hoop.
5. The last active player wins.

## Variations

- Use no-contact rules where players may only block space.
- Run team rounds with colored hoops or bibs.
- Require a specific hooping style for advanced players.

## Safety Notes

Hoops can hit faces and fingers. Keep contact light and forbid swinging hoops at people.
