> [!info] Brief Description
> A tag game where infected individuals are carried to the hospital by others and are then rescued there.

**Group Size**: 8+ players
**Difficulty**: Simple
**Materials**: 2-3 mats
**Playtime**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Two or three mats are laid out within the playing area, or small zones are marked in some other way – these are the hospitals. A number of "germs" (infected individuals) are determined based on the group size. The germ carriers try to infect healthy players by tagging them, thereby weakening them so much that they fall to the ground and lie down. Infected players can be "driven" to the hospital by two or more healthy players, accompanied by loud "nee-naw, nee-naw" sounds, and placed there. To do this, they are held by their arms and legs and carried to the mats. Healthy players who are currently acting as an ambulance, meaning they are carrying someone, are safe from the germ carriers. However, this is only true as long as the ambulance is complete. If a healthy player tries to bring someone to the hospital alone, they can be tagged. Germs naturally cannot enter the hospitals, so healthy players are safe there.
