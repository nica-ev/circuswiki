> [!info] Short Description
> One player performs and names as many valid tricks as possible within a time limit.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Poi, staff or agreed trick props, stopwatch  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

One player performs and names as many valid tricks as possible within a time limit. Another person verifies the tricks and counts the score. This is often used with poi, staff or similar skill props.

## Variations

Run separate categories for different props.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
