> [!info] Short Description
> A last-person-standing balance challenge where participants hold a handstand as long as possible.

![[../img/handstand-endurance.jpg|300]]

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: schwer  
**Material**: Soft floor, mats or grass  
**Duration**: approx. 3-10 minutes

## Game Description

All participants enter a handstand on the start signal and try to hold the agreed handstand form longer than everyone else. A player is out when their feet touch the ground, they leave the agreed area, or they break the agreed support rule.

The last person still in a valid handstand wins.

## Setup

- Choose the allowed form: free handstand, wall-supported, partner-spotted or walking handstand.
- Use a safe surface and leave enough room between participants.
- Decide whether small hand-walk corrections are allowed.

## Variations

- Run separate beginner and advanced rounds.
- Use partner-supported handstands for inclusive groups.
- Add a maximum time and declare all remaining players joint winners.

## Safety Notes

Warm up wrists and shoulders first. Do not use elimination pressure with participants who cannot exit a handstand safely.
