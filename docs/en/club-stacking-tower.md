> [!info] Short Description
> Teams build the tallest possible tower from juggling clubs.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Many clubs  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Teams build the tallest possible tower from juggling clubs. Players may hold the clubs, but the clubs must stay connected in one continuous stack. The highest stable stack at the end of the time wins.

## Variations

Require the tower to stand for three seconds after time is called if you want a stricter version.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
