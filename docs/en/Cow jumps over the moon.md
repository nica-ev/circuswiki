# Cow Jumps Over the Moon

An exercise from the second step of the [5-Step Juggling Model](Zonglowanie%20w%205%20krokach.md).

## Goal

This exercise develops bilateral coordination, builds self-confidence, and reduces anxiety through a constant sense of accomplishment. The narrative creates a playful atmosphere where mistakes are not significant, and curiosity outweighs the fear of failure.

## Props

- 1 ball
- 1 hoop

## Sequence

1. The cow jumps over the moon.
2. The cow jumps through the moon. The narrative can be changed: a fish jumps out of a pond or a rabbit out of a hat.
3. The cow is in the barn. To get out, it jumps. The gate opens, and when it jumps again, the gate closes.
4. The cow jumps from the barn to the pasture. Before this, we practice passing the hoop from hand to hand as if it were a backpack.

During the activity, participants will invent their own combinations. These can be proposed to the whole group and practiced together.
