> [!info] Short Description
> A target game where players try to throw a diabolo into a box or basket.

![[../img/game-diabolo-in-box.jpg|300]]

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: einfach  
**Material**: Diabolos, handsticks, box or basket  
**Duration**: approx. 5-10 minutes

## Game Description

Players stand behind a throwing line, spin up their diabolos and try to throw them into a box, bucket or basket placed across the hall. Throws can happen one at a time or on a shared signal.

A diabolo scores if it lands in the target and stays there. Successful throws score points or send players through to the next round. To create a final, increase the distance, reduce the target size or require several successful throws.

## Setup

- Place a box or basket at a suitable distance from the throwing line.
- Give all players enough room to throw safely.
- Decide whether players throw one at a time or together.

## Variations

- Use teams and count total successful throws.
- Let beginners stand closer and advanced players farther away.
- Use several targets with different point values.

## Safety Notes

A fixed target is safest. Avoid human-held targets unless everyone involved has explicitly agreed and the throws are controlled.
