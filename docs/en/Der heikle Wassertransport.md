> [!info] Brief Description
> A challenging cooperative task where a group transports water with ropes and a pot as loss-free as possible.

**Group Size**: 5-15 people
**Difficulty**: Challenging
**Materials**: Pot, string, water, and a second container
**Playtime**: 5-20 minutes

## **Game Description**:

To prepare for the game, tie as many ropes around a pot as there are participants. The easiest way to attach the ropes is to first tie one rope around the pot, and then attach all the other ropes to that one. Next, fill the pot with a measured amount of water and place a container a short distance away to pour the water into. Each player grabs a rope. Players must hold the rope at the very end; anyone holding the rope shorter than that breaks the rules. From this point on, the goal is to transfer the water from the pot into the container with as little spillage as possible. The pot may only be moved by the ropes being held. Everyone must keep hold of their assigned rope for the entire game. Tip: Not all ropes need to be held taut at all times. Solution: There is actually only one way to transfer the water almost completely without loss. To do this, the pot must be held securely at one side and the opposite side by the ropes attached there. At least two people, standing between those holding the pot, then swap places on opposite sides. This allows the bottom edge of the pot to be carefully pulled backward and the top edge to be carefully pulled forward when tipping. This enables the water to be poured out loss-free, but it is still not simple.
