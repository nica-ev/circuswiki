> [!info] Short Description
> Players first learn to spin a toothbrush, then try to send it into a box in the middle of the room.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: einfach  
**Material**: Toothbrushes, box or basket  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Players first learn to spin a toothbrush, then try to send it into a box in the middle of the room. The game works like a target throw after a short skill-learning phase.

## Variations

Move the box farther away in later rounds.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
