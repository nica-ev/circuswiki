> [!info] Short Description
> Two players face each other and hold clubs between their bodies, for example stomach to stomach, chest to chest and chin to chin.

**Group Size**: 2 to 20 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Three clubs per pair, three balls per juggler  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Two players face each other and hold clubs between their bodies, for example stomach to stomach, chest to chest and chin to chin. While keeping the clubs balanced, they try to juggle three balls without dropping anything. The pair that lasts longest wins.

## Variations

Start with one shared club before trying two or three. Advanced pairs can add movement.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
