> [!info] Brief Description
> A cooperative game where astronauts collect space debris through human chains and bring it back to the space station.

**Group Size**: 6-25 people
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: Marked area, objects
**Playtime**: 10-15 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A clearly defined, small area is created: the space station.
Breathing is possible without issue there.
All participants (astronauts) begin inside the space station.
The astronauts want to collect space debris located outside the space station (objects scattered around the area).
To do this, they must form chains (holding hands) to ensure their air supply.
However, this air supply is only available for 10 seconds.
If someone is outside the space station for longer than 10 seconds or is not connected to the space station by a chain, they die.
Therefore, the group must decide for themselves when to break the chains and return.

The goal is to collect all the objects.
