> [!info] Short Description
> A convention session for informal or formal juggling numbers and endurance record attempts.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Props needed for the chosen record attempt  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

A convention session can be used for an informal or formal record attempt. The group chooses a specific numbers or endurance record, defines judging rules, and gives each participant an attempt.

## Variations

Only call it a world record if the relevant record-keeping requirements are actually followed.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
