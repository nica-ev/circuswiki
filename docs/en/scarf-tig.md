> [!info] Short Description
> Every player tucks one end of a juggling scarf into the back of their clothing.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: einfach  
**Material**: One juggling scarf per player  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Every player tucks one end of a juggling scarf into the back of their clothing. Players chase each other and try to steal scarves while protecting their own. A player without a scarf is out.

## Variations

Play in teams by using scarf colors.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
