> [!info] Short Description
> Partners face each other.

**Group Size**: 2 to 20 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Diabolos and handsticks, distance markers  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Partners face each other. One player throws the diabolo upward and catches it behind the partner, close enough that the movement feels almost like reaching around them. Successful pairs repeat from farther apart.

## Variations

Use clear consent and enough space. Stop increasing distance when catches become unsafe.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
