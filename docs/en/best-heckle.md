> [!info] Short Description
> A willing performer stands in the middle and performs a simple prop skill.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: einfach  
**Material**: A willing performer and their prop  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

A willing performer stands in the middle and performs a simple prop skill. The audience tries to produce the funniest heckle without becoming hostile or personal. The best heckle wins.

## Variations

Only play this with a performer who actively wants to be teased; set tone limits first.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
