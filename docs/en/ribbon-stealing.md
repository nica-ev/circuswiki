> [!info] Short Description
> Each unicycle has a loose ribbon attached at the back.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: schwer  
**Material**: Unicycles, loose ribbons  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Each unicycle has a loose ribbon attached at the back. Riders try to steal other riders' ribbons while protecting their own. A rider is out when their ribbon is taken or when they dismount.

## Variations

Use cloth strips that release easily and never tie them tightly to the rider.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
