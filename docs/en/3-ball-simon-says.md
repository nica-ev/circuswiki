> [!info] Short Description
> A command-and-reaction game where jugglers follow only valid Simon Says instructions while keeping three balls going.

![[../img/3-ball-simon-says.jpg|300]]

**Group Size**: 3 to 50 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Three juggling balls per player  
**Duration**: approx. 5-10 minutes

## Game Description

All players juggle three balls while a caller gives movement or trick commands. Players only follow commands that begin with the agreed phrase, for example "Simon says". Anyone who follows a false command, ignores a valid command, drops, or stops juggling is out.

Possible commands include turning around, sitting down, lying down, standing up, clapping, changing pattern, moving left or right, or freezing.

## Setup

- Give each player three balls and enough personal space.
- Choose a caller.
- Agree whether players are eliminated or receive funny penalty points.

## Variations

- For beginners, use one ball or scarves.
- Make it cooperative by counting how many commands the whole group can survive.
- Let eliminated players become assistant callers.

## Safety Notes

Avoid commands that force players to dive, run into each other, or move blindly while watching their props.
