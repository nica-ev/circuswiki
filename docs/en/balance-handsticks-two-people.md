> [!info] Short Description
> Pairs balance one diabolo handstick each on their chins while the string between the sticks remains connected.

**Group Size**: 2 to 20 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Diabolo handsticks, one pair per team  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Pairs balance one diabolo handstick each on their chins while the string between the sticks remains connected. The pair that keeps the shared balance longest wins.

## Variations

Ask pairs to walk, turn or kneel in later rounds if the balance is too easy.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
