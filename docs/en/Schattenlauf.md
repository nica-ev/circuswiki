> [!info] Brief Description
> A partner movement game where one person imitates the other's movements like a shadow.

**Group Size**: 2+ people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Duration**: Variable

## **Game Description**:
Form pairs.
One partner starts and moves freely around the space (moving arms and legs, dancing, etc.).
The other person imitates the partner's movements, thus becoming their shadow.
After a set amount of time, switch roles.
