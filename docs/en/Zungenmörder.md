> [!info] Brief Description
> A circle game where a murderer secretly "kills" by sticking out their tongue, and a detective must unmask them.

**Group Size**: 10-30 people
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

All players sit together in a circle and close their eyes.

While this is happening, the game leader walks around the circle and taps one person *once* on the back (this is the murderer) and another person *twice* (this is the detective).

Afterward, everyone opens their eyes again, and the detective sits in the middle of the circle.

Now, everyone looks at each other. If the murderer looks at another person, they discreetly stick their tongue out at them.

The targeted person must then "die" dramatically.

The detective can make a total of 3 guesses as to who the murderer might be.

If they successfully identify the murderer, the detective wins.

If they guess incorrectly, the murderer wins.
