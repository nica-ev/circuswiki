> [!info] Short Description
> A team version of gladiators where the surviving player scores for their team.

**Group Size**: 6 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Juggling clubs or agreed gladiator props  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Players are split into equal teams and play a gladiators round. When only one player remains, their team scores a point. If several players from the same team survive together, the team can receive extra points.

## Variations

Use team tactics and short repeat rounds rather than one long elimination.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
