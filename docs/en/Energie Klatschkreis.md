> [!info] Brief Description
> A fast-paced circle game where a clapping impulse is passed from person to person as smoothly as possible.

**Group Size**: 5+ people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: 2-5 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A circle game for small to large groups.

Everyone stands in a circle, and a clap is passed to the neighbor. The goal is to receive this clap, turn around, and pass it on – as quickly as possible.
