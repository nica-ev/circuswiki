> [!info] Brief Description
> A quiet perception game where a person lying down with their eyes closed guesses the number of hands placed on them.

**Group Size**: 4-25 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A relaxation game suitable for any group size.

Form groups of 4-5 people.

One person lies flat on their stomach and closes their eyes.

The others non-verbally agree on how many hands they will place on the lying person's body simultaneously.

The person on the floor must then guess how many hands are on their body.

Depending on time and the participants' comfort, several rounds can be played. Afterwards, roles are switched.
