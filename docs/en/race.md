> [!info] Short Description
> Players race to a chosen destination and back while continuously juggling.

**Group Size**: 3 to 60 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Juggling props chosen by participants, route markers  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Players race to a chosen destination and back while continuously juggling. If a player drops, either they are out immediately or they must wait through a short time penalty before continuing. The first player to return with a valid juggling run wins.

## Variations

Use any prop that fits the group level. For safety, use a short marked course instead of a real-world route when working with children or mixed groups.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
