> [!info] Brief Description
> A game of Rock-Paper-Scissors where losers become fans and form a growing conga line.

**Group Size**: 20+ participants
**Difficulty**: Very Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 8 minutes

## **Game Description**:

All players move around the room and find an opponent to play Rock-Paper-Scissors with, using only scissors, rock, and paper! The loser becomes a fan of the winner. The loser holds onto the winner's shoulders, follows them, and calls out their name the entire time. If someone who already has fans plays and loses, that player and all their fans become fans of the player who won. They all cheer them on in a long conga line. This continues until only one winner remains, whom everyone cheers for.
