> [!info] Short Description
> Each player contributes three coins and juggles them.

![[../img/game-moneyjuggling_prize.jpg|300]]

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Three coins per player, collection hat  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Each player contributes three coins and juggles them. When a player drops, they put their coins into a central hat or pot. The last player still juggling wins the collected coins.

## Variations

Use tokens instead of real money for children or public workshops.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
