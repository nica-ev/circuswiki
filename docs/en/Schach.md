> [!info] Brief Description
> A silent tactical game for two teams where participants jump, strike, and aim to reach the baseline like game pieces.

**Group Size**: 6-20 people
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Suitable for small to medium group sizes.

Two equally sized teams are formed.
A rectangular playing field is designated, with each team lining up on opposite edges.

The following rules apply:
* Turns are taken alternately.
* Only one person from a team may take a turn at a time.
* A turn consists of jumping with both feet and landing safely on both feet. A lunge or any other unstable movement/landing means the player must leave the playing field.
* The decision of who jumps from the team can only be made through eye contact; no talking or gesturing is allowed.
* If multiple players jump simultaneously, the game leader must decide who has to leave the playing field.
* Opponent's pieces can be "captured" by touching them with your hand after landing safely. These players must then leave the playing field.
* The team that first reaches the opponent's baseline or captures all opposing pieces wins.
