> [!info] Brief Description
> A circle game with a ball, where a bomb explodes after a secret countdown, creating obstacles within the circle.

**Group Size**: 6-30 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: Ball
**Game Duration**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Everyone stands in a circle, with one participant sitting in the middle.
The participant in the middle quietly counts from 1 to 10, then loudly shouts "Bomb!"

Meanwhile, a ball is passed around the circle as quickly as possible (important: don't throw, just pass).
The person holding the ball when "Bomb!" is called is out, and sits down on the spot with their legs stretched out.

The person to their right/left must then jump over their legs to pass the ball.

The person in the middle starts counting from 1 again and again, until only one participant remains in the circle.
This person is the winner.
