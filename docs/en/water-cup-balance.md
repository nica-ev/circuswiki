> [!info] Short Description
> Players balance a club or similar object with a plastic cup of water on top.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: mittel  
**Material**: Club or balance object, plastic cup of water  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Players balance a club or similar object with a plastic cup of water on top. If the object falls or the water spills beyond the agreed limit, the player is out.

## Variations

Use only a small amount of water indoors.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
