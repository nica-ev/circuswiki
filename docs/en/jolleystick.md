> [!info] Short Description
> Two players play a net game with a devil stick.

**Group Size**: 2 to 20 players  
**Difficulty**: schwer  
**Material**: Devil stick, handstick, net or court line  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Two players play a net game with a devil stick. The devil stick is sent back and forth across the net using one handstick, similar to volleyball. Players need controlled propellor throws to keep the rally alive.

## Variations

Use a low net and short court for first attempts.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
