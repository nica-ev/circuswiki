> [!info] Short Description
> A themed juggling knockout game where three-ball jugglers try to survive a zombie-style chase.

![[../img/3-ball-zombie-knockout.jpg|300]]

**Group Size**: 5 to 40 players
**Difficulty**: mittel
**Material**: Three juggling balls per active player, marked arena
**Duration**: approx. 5-10 minutes

## Game Description

Players juggle three balls while avoiding zombie pressure. The game combines juggling endurance, tag, and a playful horror theme.

## Setup

- Mark the arena.
- Choose one or more starting zombies.
- Define zombie movement limits, such as slow walking, arms forward, or no running.

## Rules

1. All non-zombie players start juggling three balls.
2. Zombies move through the arena and try to tag or pressure jugglers within the agreed limits.
3. A juggler becomes a zombie or is eliminated when they drop, leave the arena, or are legally tagged.
4. The round continues until one non-zombie juggler remains or all players have become zombies.
5. The last surviving juggler wins, or the zombies win if everyone is converted.

## Variations

- Use conversion instead of elimination so everyone keeps playing.
- Let zombies carry no props for beginner safety.
- Require zombies to juggle one ball for an advanced version.

## Safety Notes

Keep zombie movement slow and predictable. Do not allow grabbing, tackling, or jump scares in crowded groups.
