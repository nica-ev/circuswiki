> [!info] Short Description
> Longest passing distance wins.

**Group Size**: 2 to 20 players  
**Difficulty**: schwer  
**Material**: Six clubs per pair, distance markers  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Pairs begin close together with a six-club passing pattern. While passing, they move farther apart. When a pair drops, they stop at that distance. The pair with the greatest clean distance wins.

## Variations

Instead of measuring final distance, set a finish line and let the first pair to pass across it win.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
