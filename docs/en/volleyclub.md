> [!info] Short Description
> Volleyclub is played like volleyball with clubs.

**Group Size**: 6 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: schwer  
**Material**: Clubs, volleyball net or marked net line  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Volleyclub is played like volleyball with clubs. Players juggle or hold two clubs, pass between teammates, and send a club over the net. If the other team lets a club drop or fails to return it legally, the throwing team scores.

## Variations

Use three players per side on a full court for an advanced version, or smaller teams and a low net for workshops.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
