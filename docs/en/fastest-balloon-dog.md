> [!info] Short Description
> Players race to a balloon pump, inflate a balloon and make a balloon dog as quickly as possible.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: einfach  
**Material**: Balloons, pumps, race markers  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Players race to a balloon pump, inflate a balloon and make a balloon dog as quickly as possible. The first player to finish a recognizable dog and hold it up wins.

## Variations

Use another simple balloon model if dogs are not known by the group.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
