> [!info] Short Description
> A convention knockout game where unicyclists try to stay mounted while forcing others to dismount.

![[../img/game-ewan-unicycle-gladiators.jpg|300]]

**Group Size**: 4 to 60 players  
**Difficulty**: schwer  
**Material**: Unicycles, marked arena  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Unicyclists ride inside a marked arena. The goal is to stay mounted longer than everyone else while applying controlled pressure to the other riders.

A rider is out when they dismount, touch the floor with a foot or hand, leave the arena, or use illegal contact. Eliminated riders leave the arena immediately. The last rider still mounted wins.

## Setup

- Mark a clear riding area with enough space around the edges.
- Agree on contact rules before the round starts.
- Make sure all participants can ride confidently before joining.

## Variations

- Run a no-contact beginner version where riders may only block space, not touch.
- Use team colors and count points for the team of the last remaining rider.
- Shrink the arena between rounds for experienced riders.

## Safety Notes

Use only with confident unicyclists. Define contact limits clearly. For public use, keep contact to light shoulder or arm pressure and forbid grabs, wheel blocks and body checks.
