> [!info] Brief Description
> A line-based movement game where the group stays connected and lies down one after another like a snake.

**Group Size**: 5-20 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A simple movement game for small to medium-sized groups.

Everyone stands in a line with their legs spread apart.
Everyone bends forward so they can reach through their own legs with their right hand and grasp the left hand of the person behind them.

The last person in line lies down on the ground (hands can be released).
The others now walk together (without letting go of hands) towards the back until the "new" last person has space to lie down.

The game ends when everyone is lying down.

## **Variations**:

As a variation, you can also close the game into an endless circle by having the lying-down people stand up again once everyone else has walked over them, and then rejoin the line at the front.
