> [!info] Brief Description
> A short icebreaker exercise in a circle where names are linked with individual movements.

**Group Size**: 4 or more participants
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Duration**: 2-10 minutes (depending on the number of participants)

## **Game Description**:
Everyone stands in a circle. One by one, a person steps into the center of the circle, greets the others with a unique movement, and introduces themselves.

*E.g.: "I am Marc" + doing a forward roll*

The person steps back into the circle. Now, everyone else imitates the movement and greets them back:

--> Everyone does a forward roll and says "Hello Marc"*
