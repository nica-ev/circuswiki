> [!info] Brief Description
> A sneaking and cooperation game where a group secretly steals and returns an object.

**Group Size**: 5-25 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: Small object
**Game Duration**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

One person stands with their back to the others. A small object (e.g., a ball) is placed behind them.

Whenever this person turns around, the others must freeze. Anyone who moves must take two steps back (or return to the start).

The goal is to steal the ball and bring it to the starting line.
As soon as the ball is taken, the "Ice Queen" can make ONE guess as to who currently has the ball.

If she guesses correctly, she wins.
