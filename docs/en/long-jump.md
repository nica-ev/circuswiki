> [!info] Short Description
> Unicyclists line up at a takeoff mark and jump as far as possible.

**Group Size**: 2 to 40 players  
**Difficulty**: schwer  
**Material**: Unicycles, takeoff marker, measuring tape  
**Duration**: approx. 5-15 minutes

## Game Description

Unicyclists line up at a takeoff mark and jump as far as possible. The jump only counts if the rider lands and stays on the unicycle.

## Variations

Many riders prefer to jump sideways from the line; define allowed technique before starting.

## Safety Notes

Keep the playing area clear and define the limits before the round starts. For contact games, target props rather than bodies. For throwing, riding, balancing or acrobatic games, leave enough space for failed attempts and stop the round if the group starts taking unsafe risks.
